# Key facts on S Pass

Get an overview and key facts about the S Pass for hiring foreign skilled workers (Associate Professionals and Technicians (APTs)) to work in Singapore.

## Pass facts

| Who it is for | Skilled workers who meet the [eligibility criteria](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/eligibility) . | 
|---|---|
| Who can apply | An employer or appointed employment agent must apply on behalf of the applicant. If the pass holder changes jobs, the new employer has to [apply for a new pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/apply-for-a-pass) . | 
| Qualifying salary |  [Fixed monthly salary](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/employment-pass/what-is-a-fixed-monthly-salary) comparable to top one-third of local APT salaries. This is a minimum of $3,300 (and increases progressively with age, up to $4,800 for those in mid-40s). Candidates in the [financial service sector](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/employment-pass/how-do-i-know-if-my-company-is-considered-part-of-the-financial-services-sector) need higher salaries to qualify. The S Pass minimum qualifying salary will increase from $3,300 to $3,600 for new applications from 1 January 2027 and renewals expiring from 1 January 2028. | 
| What is the duration |  **Note:**  The duration granted is limited by your passport validity. The pass can only be valid up to 1 month before your passport expiry date. | 
| Is it renewable | Yes. Find out [how to renew](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/renew-a-pass) . | 
| Quota or levy | Yes. Employers are subject to [a quota and levy](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/quota-and-levy) for S Pass employees. | 
| Medical insurance | Employers must provide [medical insurance](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/medical-insurance) for S Pass holders. | 
| Primary Care Plan (PCP) | Employers must complete **all** the following:  | 
| Relevant legislation | [Conditions of Work Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/services-forms/passes/wpspassconditions.pdf)[Employment of Foreign Manpower Act](https://www.mom.gov.sg/legislation/employment-of-foreign-manpower-act) | 
| Related eServices and forms | Access [online services and forms](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/eservices-and-forms) for S Pass. |

## Pass map

Get an idea of what you need to do before, during and after you apply for an S Pass.

## Before applying

- 
  - 
 Advertise on MyCareersFuture and [consider all candidates fairly](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/consider-all-candidates-fairly) .
  - 
 Know the [eligibility and requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/eligibility) to apply.
  - 
 Understand the S Pass [quota and levy requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/quota-and-levy) .
  - 
  If you have never applied for an S Pass, you need to [declare your business activity](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/declare-your-business-activity) .*For first-time employers*
  - 
 Find out the [documents required](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/documents-required) for applying.
- 

## Before candidate's arrival

- 
  - 
  [Apply for the S Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/apply-for-a-pass) and wait for the outcome.
  - 
 (If your pass is approved) Get an in-principle approval letter.
  - 
  Buy the [Primary Care Plan](https://www.mom.gov.sg/primary-care-plan) . *For workers who stay in dormitories or work in the construction, marine shipyard, and process (CMP) sectors*
  - 
 Ensure that the candidate complies with the latest [travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore) .
- 

## Upon candidate's arrival

- 
  - 
 Buy [medical insurance](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/medical-insurance) for each pass holder and submit the insurance details online.
  - 
  [Get the pass issued](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/apply-for-a-pass#get-the-pass-issued) and receive a notification letter stating whether registration is required.
  - 
 (If required) Make an appointment for the candidate to [register their fingerprints and photo](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/apply-for-a-pass#register-fingerprints-and-photo) .
  - 
  [Receive the pass card](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/apply-for-a-pass#receive-the-card) at the address provided 4 working days after registration or document verification.
- 

## As and when required

- 
  - 
  [Notify MOM](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/notify-mom-of-changes) if the company's or pass holder's information changes.
  - 
  [Renew the pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/renew-a-pass) before it expires.
  - 
  [Apply for a replacement](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/replace-a-pass-card) if the pass card is lost, stolen or damaged.
  - 
  [Cancel the pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/cancel-a-pass) if the pass holder no longer works for you.
