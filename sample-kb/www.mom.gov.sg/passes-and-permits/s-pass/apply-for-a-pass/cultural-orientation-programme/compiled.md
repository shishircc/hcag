# Cultural Orientation Programme (COP) for S Pass holders

## At a glance

| Who should attend | S Pass holders who are working in Singapore for the first time | 
|---|---|
| When to attend | After the pass application is approved They are encouraged to complete the programme before they start work.  | 
| What is the duration | 3 online modules, 30 minutes each | 
| How much it costs | Free | 
| How to register | [Register online](https://cop.mom.gov.sg) | 
| Language used in the COP | English | 

## What is the Cultural Orientation Programme (COP)

The COP is launched as a pilot and is an online learning experience for S Pass holders to:

- Familiarise themselves with Singapore’s culture and workplace norms
- Encourage positive interactions with locals

S Pass holders are encouraged to complete the COP before they start work. The programme covers the following topics:

- Expectations, rights and responsibilities on living and working in Singapore
- How to create positive interactions with colleagues and the community
- Appreciating and understanding Singapore’s culture
- Resources available to learn and find out more after completing the online programme

After the pilot ends in 2025, MOM will consider including the COP as part of the S Pass application process.

If you would like to provide feedback or require assistance, please [contact us](https://www.mom.gov.sg/contact-us).
