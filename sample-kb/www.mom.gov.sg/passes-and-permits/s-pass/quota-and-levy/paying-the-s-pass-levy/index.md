# Paying the levy for S Pass

You will have to pay a levy for your S Pass holder every month. Find out how you can pay and the penalties for non-payment or late payment.

## When and how to pay levy

You must pay the levy for each month by the 17th of the following month (or the next working day if it falls on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday). The payment due date is stated in your levy bill.

For a convenient and hassle-free payment, you can [apply for GIRO](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/levy/how-do-i-apply-for-giro-for-my-levy-payment). If you do not have an existing GIRO arrangement to pay levy, you can [pay using PayNow QR](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/levy/how-do-i-pay-my-foreign-workers-levy-if-my-giro-application-has-not-been-approved). 

| After GIRO is approved |  If the [GIRO deduction is unsuccessful](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/levy/what-should-i-do-if-my-giro-deduction-for-levy-payment-was-unsuccessful) , your next levy bill may be higher than usual due to penalty charges incurred for the late payment.  | 
|---|---|
| If you need to pay levy [using PayNow QR](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/levy/how-do-i-pay-my-foreign-workers-levy-if-my-giro-application-has-not-been-approved) | You must pay the levy for each month **by the 17<sup>th</sup> of the following month** (or the next working day if it falls on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday). | 

## View levy bills online

You can [view your latest levy bill](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/check-and-pay-levy) on the 3<sup>rd</sup> working day of every month.

## Penalties for not paying levy

If you do not pay the **full levy on time**, you may face these penalties:

- You will be charged a [late payment penalty](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/levy/how-are-levy-penalty-charges-calculated) .
- Your existing Work Permits and S Passes will be revoked the following month if you miss levy payments for two consecutive months.
 **Note:** After clearing the arrears and fines, you may submit a new application for S Pass holders. Your S Pass holders must obtain new S Passes before they can continue to work.
- You will not be allowed to apply for S Passes.
- You may face legal action to recover the unpaid levy.
- If you, your partners or directors head other companies, these companies may not be allowed to apply for S Passes.
