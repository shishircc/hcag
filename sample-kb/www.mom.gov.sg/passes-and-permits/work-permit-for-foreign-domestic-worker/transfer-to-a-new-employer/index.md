# Transfer a migrant domestic worker (MDW) directly to a new employer

To transfer an MDW directly, both the new and current employers must carry out certain steps.

## If you are the current employer

You need to:

1. Send the helper for her [6-monthly medical examination](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/six-monthly-medical-examination) if you have not done so.
2. Log in to our [FDW eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies) to approve the transfer request from the new employer. Please**wait** for the request which will be emailed to you before you log in to approve it.
3. [Pay levy](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/levy/do-i-still-need-to-pay-levy-even-though-i-have-sent-my-fdw-back-to-the-employment-agency-to-transfer-her-to-another-employer) up till the date the new employer issues the Work Permit.
 
Once the new Work Permit is issued, the current one will be automatically cancelled and your levy billing will stop.
4. 
Cut the cancelled Work Permit card in half and discard it to prevent misuse.

## If you are the new employer

You need to carry out these steps in sequence.

**Note:** The first 2 steps must be completed **30 days** before the helper’s Work Permit expires.

| Step | When to do it | Result | 
|---|---|---|
| **Before you submit an application** Check with the current employer that the helper has gone for her [6-monthly medical examination](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/six-monthly-medical-examination) . | At least 30 days before the helper’s Work Permit expires | Ensure the helper  | 
| **Submit an application**  Log in to our [FDW eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies) to request for the current employer’s consent for the transfer. Once consent is given, you can proceed to [apply for the helper’s Work Permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies) . | At least 30 days before the helper’s Work Permit expires | Receive outcome within 1 week. If the application is approved, we will email you the in-principle approval (IPA). Employers can also log in to our [FDW eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies) to download the IPA and declaration form. | 
| **Prepare for helper's transfer** Complete the steps on the IPA. Log in to our [FDW eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies) to print the Declaration form. Both you and the helper need to sign it. | When you receive the IPA | You will need to:  | 
| **Get the permit issued** Contact the current employer to arrange the date for the helper’s transfer. On the **agreed transfer date** , use our[FDW eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies) to do the following:  | Agreed transfer date | Give the Temporary Work Permit to the helper so that she can start working for you. Her current Work Permit will be automatically cancelled. | 

The due date to get the new Work Permit issued is one of the following dates, whichever is earlier:

- 21 days from Work Permit approval
- Current Work Permit expiry date

Otherwise, an overstaying fine will be imposed.

## Change the transfer date

*Processing time: Immediate*

As the new employer, you can request to change the transfer date if both you and the current employer agree to it.

You can delay the transfer date by:

1. Extending the IPA expiry date using our [FDW eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies) .
2. Print the updated IPA letter which will show the new due date for the Work Permit issuance.
