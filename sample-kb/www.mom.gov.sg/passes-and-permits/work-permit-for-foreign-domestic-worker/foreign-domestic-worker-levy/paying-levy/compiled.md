# Paying levy for a migrant domestic worker (MDW)

You have to pay a levy for your helper every month. Find out the levy rates, how you can pay and the penalties for non-payment or late payment.

## What is the foreign worker levy

The foreign worker levy, commonly known as “levy”, is a pricing mechanism to regulate the number of foreigners in Singapore.

As an employer, you don’t have to pay Central Provident Fund (CPF) contributions for your helper. However, you must pay a monthly levy for her.

[For first-time helpers](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/fdw-eligibility#criteria-for-first-time-helpers), the levy will begin on the
**5th day of their arrival** (including the arrival date). Otherwise, the levy will begin **the next day** after the helper arrives.

The levy ends when the Work Permit is cancelled or expires.

## Levy rate

The levy rate for helpers is as follows:

| Type of levy | Monthly rate | Daily rate | 
|---|---|---|
| Normal (1 <sup>st</sup> helper) | $300 | $9.87 | 
| Normal (Subsequent helpers) | $450 | $14.80 | 
| Concessionary | $60 | $1.98 | 

**Note:**

- The daily levy rate only applies to helpers who did not complete a full calendar month. The daily levy rate is calculated as follows: (Monthly levy rate x 12) / 365 = rounding up to the nearest cent.
- The concessionary rate applies to families with a young child, elderly member or person with disability. Find out if you qualify for [levy concession](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/foreign-domestic-worker-levy/levy-concession) .
- When [you are no longer eligible for levy concession or your number of helpers changes](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/levy/why-did-my-helpers-levy-rate-change) , the levy rate may be adjusted. The new rate takes effect immediately.

## When and how to pay levy

You must pay the levy for each month by the 17th of the following month (or the next working day if it falls on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday). The payment due date is stated in your levy bill.

For a convenient and hassle-free payment, you can [apply for GIRO](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/levy/how-do-i-apply-for-giro-for-my-levy-payment). If you do not have an existing GIRO arrangement to pay levy, you can [pay using PayNow QR](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/levy/how-do-i-pay-my-foreign-workers-levy-if-my-giro-application-has-not-been-approved).

| After GIRO is approved |  If the [GIRO deduction is unsuccessful](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/levy/what-should-i-do-if-my-giro-deduction-for-levy-payment-was-unsuccessful) , your next levy bill may be higher than usual due to penalty charges incurred for the late payment.  | 
|---|---|
| If you need to pay levy [using PayNow QR](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/levy/how-do-i-pay-my-foreign-workers-levy-if-my-giro-application-has-not-been-approved) | You must pay the levy for each month **by the 17<sup>th</sup> of the following month** (or the next working day if it falls on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday). | 

## View levy bills online

You can [view your latest levy bill](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/check-and-pay-levy) on the 3<sup>rd</sup> working day of every month.

- You have not set up a GIRO account.
- Your GIRO account has changed.
- Your helper details have changed.
- The amount payable is different from the earlier month.

## Penalties for not paying levy

If you do not pay the full levy on time, you may face the following penalties:

- You will be charged a [late payment penalty](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/levy/how-are-levy-penalty-charges-calculated) .
- Your helper's Work Permit will be revoked the following month if you miss levy payments for two consecutive months.
- You will not be allowed to apply for Work Permits.
- You may face legal action to recover the unpaid levy.
