# Employer requirements for hiring an MDW

As an employer, you need to meet the eligibility criteria before hiring a migrant domestic worker (MDW).

## Who is eligible

To hire a helper, you must:

- Be 21 years old and above.
- Not be an undischarged bankrupt.
- Have the mental capacity to fully understand and discharge your responsibility as an employer.

We will also consider your care-giving needs (e.g. young children or elderly family members) and financial ability to hire, maintain and upkeep the helper in an acceptable accommodation.

If you are a **first-time employer of a helper**, you must attend an [Employer Orientation Programme](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/employers-orientation-programme-eop) (EOP) before you can apply for a Work Permit.
