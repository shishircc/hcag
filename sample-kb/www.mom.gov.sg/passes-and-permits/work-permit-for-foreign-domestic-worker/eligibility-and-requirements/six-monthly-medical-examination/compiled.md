# Six-monthly medical examination (6ME) for MDWs

During your migrant domestic worker's (MDW) employment, you must send her for a medical screening every 6 months. This medical examination screens for pregnancy and infectious diseases such as syphilis, HIV and tuberculosis.

## Requirements

The 6ME test results must be certified by a Singapore-registered doctor. You must pay for the 6ME and any medical expenses arising from it.

|  | Details | 
|---|---|
| **Tests conducted during 6ME** Our 6ME letters will state the tests your helper needs to do. |  | 
| **Which 6ME results must be submitted to MOM?** | All 6ME results must be submitted to MOM. This is regardless of the tests helpers need to take. | 
| **Who can submit 6ME results?** | All results must be submitted by clinics. Employers can use our [FDW eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies) to check if clinics have submitted their helpers' results and view the results. | 
| **Where can 6ME be conducted?** | All 6MEs must be conducted in clinics. Employers or their representatives should not be present during the examination. | 

For more details, please refer to [our FAQs](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/work-passes-and-permits/faqs-for-enhanced-6me.pdf). 

## When to send your MDW for 6ME

You will receive a **notification letter and 6ME form by post** when your helper's 6ME is due. She must complete her 6ME by the due date. 

[FDW eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies)to check her previous 6ME date. You can then know when her next one is due, and also reprint the 6ME form.

## If your MDW is 50 years old or above

If your helper is 50 years old or above, she doesn’t need to go for 6ME.

She only needs to go for a medical examination upon renewal of her Work Permit.

## If your MDW is on overseas leave

If your helper is due for her 6ME while she is [away on overseas leave](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/employers-guide/overseas-leave-for-fdw), you can [request to postpone](https://www.mom.gov.sg/defer-waive-mdw-6ME) it until she returns. 

## If your MDW’s employment is ending soon

If you are sending your helper home within 1 month from her 6ME due date, you can choose not to send her for the 6ME.

### When to apply for waiver

If you intend to keep her for **more than 1 month** from her 6ME due date, you need to [apply for a waiver](https://www.mom.gov.sg/defer-waive-mdw-6ME). 

## If your MDW fails the 6ME

If your helper did not pass her 6ME, you must [cancel her Work Permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/cancel-a-work-permit) and send her home immediately.
