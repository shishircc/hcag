# Get a short Work Permit extension for an MDW

You can request for a short extension of your migrant domestic worker (MDW)'s Work Permit if you need more time before renewal or cancellation.

You can request for a 4-week extension of your helper's Work Permit if you:

- Need more time before renewing her Work Permit (e.g. buying security bond or her medical and personal accident insurance).
- Plan to cancel the Work Permit but need your helper to work for a short period beyond the Work Permit expiry date (e.g. transferring her to another employer and are waiting for her new Work Permit application to be approved).

Log in to our [FDW eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies) to request for the extension. You must do so **within 2 months before the Work Permit expires**.

If you don't intend to keep employing your helper, please remember to [cancel her Work Permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/cancel-a-work-permit) **before the extended expiry date.** Otherwise, your security bond may be forfeited.

**Additional step for employment agencies:**Please get the employer’s authorisation using our

[FDW eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies)

**before you can perform any transactions for them**. For more details, you can refer to

[our FAQs](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/work-passes-and-permits/faq-2fa.pdf)and

[infographic](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/work-passes-and-permits/ea-infographic.pdf).
