# Key facts on Work Permit for MDW

Get an overview and key facts about the Work Permit for a migrant domestic worker (MDW).

## Pass facts

| Who it is for | Foreigners employed as domestic helpers in Singapore. Both employers and helpers must meet the [eligibility criteria](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements) . | 
|---|---|
| Who can apply | Employer, sponsor or authorised employment agent needs to [apply for the Work Permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/apply-for-work-permit) . | 
| Qualifying salary | No minimum required. | 
| What is the duration | Up to 2 years, depending on the validity of the security bond. | 
| Is it renewable | Yes. Find out [how to renew](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/renew-a-work-permit) . | 
| Foreign worker levy | Yes. Employers must pay a [monthly levy](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/foreign-domestic-worker-levy) . | 
| Medical insurance | Employers must provide [medical and personal accident insurance](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/insurance-requirements) . | 
| Relevant legislation | [Conditions of Work Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/services-forms/passes/wpspassconditions.pdf)[Employment of Foreign Manpower Act](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/EFMA1990) | 
| Related eServices and forms | Access [online services and forms](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eservices-and-forms) for helpers. | 

## Pass map

Get an idea of what you need to do before, during and after you apply for a Work Permit.

## Before applying

- 
  - 
  Know the [eligibility and requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements) to apply.
  - 
  Understand the [guidelines](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/employers-guide) to help create a good working relationship with your MDW.*For employers*
  - 
  Register for the [Employers' Orientation Programme](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/employers-orientation-programme-eop) .*For first-time employers*
- 

## Before MDW's arrival

- 
### Apply for Work Permit
  - 
  Know the [eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/apply-under-a-special-scheme) to apply under the Joint-Income or Sponsorship schemes.*Lower-income employers or senior citizens may be eligible*
  - 
  [Apply for the Work Permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/apply-for-work-permit) . Once approved, you will get an in-principle approval to bring the MDW into Singapore.
- 
- 
### Prepare for MDW's arrival
  - 
 Ensure that the MDW complies with the latest [travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore) .
  - 
  Buy a [security bond](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/security-bond) .*For non-Malaysian MDW*
  - 
  Buy [medical and personal accident insurance](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/insurance-requirements) for the MDW and submit the insurance details online.
  - 
  Register the MDW for the [Settling-in Programme (SIP)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/settling-in-programme-sip) once you know their arrival date.*For first-time MDWs*
- 

## Upon MDW's arrival

- 
### Before permit can be issued
  - 
  Send the MDW to attend the [SIP](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/settling-in-programme-sip) within 7 days of their arrival in Singapore, excluding Sunday and public holidays.*For first-time MDWs*
  - 
  Send the MDW for a [medical examination](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/medical-examination) by a Singapore-registered doctor within 2 weeks of arrival.
- 
- 
### Get the Work Permit issued
  - 
  When the worker arrives, you can get the [permit issued](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/apply-for-work-permit#get-the-permit-issued) and you will get a Temporary Work Permit stating whether registration is required.
  - 
 (If required) Make an appointment for the MDW to [register their fingerprints and photo](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/apply-for-work-permit#register-fingerprints-and-photo) .
  - 
  [Receive the Work Permit card](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/apply-for-work-permit#receive-the-card) at the address provided 4 working days after registration or document verification.
- 

## Monthly

- 
### Every month
  - 
  [Pay the MDW levy](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/foreign-domestic-worker-levy/paying-levy) by GIRO.
- 
- 
### Every 6 months
  - 
  Send the MDW for a [medical examination](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/six-monthly-medical-examination) every 6 months.
- 

## As and when required

- 
  - 
  [Notify MOM](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/notify-mom-of-changes) if you or your MDW's information changes.
  - 
  You can [apply for levy waiver](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/foreign-domestic-worker-levy/apply-for-levy-waiver) in certain situations.
  - 
  [Renew the Work Permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/renew-a-work-permit) before it expires.
  - 
  [Apply for a replacement](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/replace-a-card) if the permit card is lost, stolen or damaged.
  - 
  [Cancel the permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/cancel-a-work-permit) if the MDW no longer works for you.
