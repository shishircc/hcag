## Salary and paymentShow

Find out what to do if anyone demands kickbacks from you:

- [What are kickbacks](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/what-are-kickbacks.pdf) – It is illegal for employers or anyone to demand money from their foreign employees in exchange for keeping their jobs. Also available in[Bengali](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/what-are-kickbacks-bengali.pdf) ,[Mandarin](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/what-are-kickbacks-mandarin.pdf) ,[Tamil](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/what-are-kickbacks-tamil.pdf) ,[Thai](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/what-are-kickbacks-thai.pdf) and[Vietnamese](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/what-are-kickbacks-vietnamese.pdf) .
- [Collecting kickbacks is a serious offence](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/collecting-kickbacks-is-a-serious-offence.pdf) – Stern action will be taken against those found to have collected kickbacks. Also available in[Bengali](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/collecting-kickbacks-is-a-serious-offence-bengali.pdf) ,[Mandarin](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/collecting-kickbacks-is-a-serious-offence-mandarin.pdf) ,[Tamil](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/collecting-kickbacks-is-a-serious-offence-tamil.pdf) ,[Thai](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/collecting-kickbacks-is-a-serious-offence-thai.pdf) and[Vietnamese](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/collecting-kickbacks-is-a-serious-offence-vietnamese.pdf) .
- [Do not pay to keep your job - Do not pay for employment costs](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/kickback-infographic-english.pdf) . Also available in[Bengali](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/kickback-infographic-bengali.pdf) ,[Burmese](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/kickback-infographic-vietnamese.pdf) ,[Mandarin](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/kickback-infographic-mandarin.pdf) ,[Tamil](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/kickback-infographic-tamil.pdf) and[Thai](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/kickback-infographic-thai.pdf) .
- [Reporting kickbacks](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/report-kickbacks-practices.pdf) – Migrant workers who have paid, or have been asked to pay kickbacks to anyone should report to MOM. Also available in[Bengali](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/reporting-kickbacks-bengali.pdf) ,[Mandarin](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/reporting-kickbacks-mandarin.pdf) ,[Tamil,](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/reporting-kickbacks-tamil.pdf)[Thai](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/reporting-kickbacks-thai.pdf) and[Vietnamese](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/reporting-kickbacks-vietnamese.pdf) .
- [How to report on kickbacks](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/how-to-report-on-kickbacks.pdf) - Available channels for reporting on kickbacks.
- [My employment, my employer pays](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/my-employment-my-employer-pays.pdf) – Your employer must bear the costs related to employing you.
- [Salary on time, every time](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/salary-on-time-every-time.pdf) – Know when and how you should get your salary and overtime pay. Also available in[Bengali / Mandarin](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/salary-on-time-bengali-mandarin.pdf) and[English / Tamil](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/salary-on-time-english-tamil.pdf) .
