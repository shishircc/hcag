# **OnE wORk pASS, OnE jOB** 一张工作证，一份工作 



**You must carry your original work permit card at all times and produce it for inspection on demand by any public officer.** 任何时候你都必须随身携带工作证正本，并应任何公职人员的检查要求而出示它。 

**You must have a valid work pass to work in your job. You can work only in the occupation and for the employer specified in your work pass.** 你必须持有一张有效的工作证，才能投入工作。你只能够从事工作准证上 所注明的工作， 并受聘于工作准证上所注明的雇主。 



**You must not be self-employed, participate in any business or hold a second job to earn extra money.** 你绝对不可自雇、参与任何生意或兼职第二份工作以赚取外快。 



## **Remember! It is illegal to work for another employer.** 切记！为另一名雇主工作是违法的。
