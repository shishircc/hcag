# **Do Not Pay to Keep your Job Do Not Pay for Employment Costs** 



**It is illegal for your employer to demand that you pay for:** 





Work Pass Security Renewal Bond 



Medical Insurance 



Repatriation Costs 







Compulsory Medical Training Examination Fees 

Levy Payment 



<!-- Start of picture text -->
8<br><!-- End of picture text -->

Guarantee for Employment 

**If your employer compels you to pay a fee or deducts it from your salary:** 





Take note of the payment details (e.g. when, how much, who did you pay to) 

Collect documents, photos or videos as evidence, where possible 



## **Report to MOM immediately.** 



Call Ministry of Manpower (MOM) at 64385122 Report on MOM Website: <u>www.mom.gov.sg/feedback</u> 

Walk in to MOM Services Centre, 1500 Bendemeer Road, Singapore 339946 



**If your claim is valid:** 



MOM will refer you to our employment agency (EA) partners, to help you find a new job 

No EA fees required
