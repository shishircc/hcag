# **Play your part to update your contact details and report overcrowding** 

**Inform your employer immediately when there are changes to  your residential address or contact number so that we can reach you in times of emergency.** 



**Enforcement action will be taken against work pass holders who failed to provide accurate contact  information.** 



**Overcrowding may cause potential safety and health concerns.** 

**If there are more than 6 persons staying at your flat or apartment, alert MOM at 6438 5122 or www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/ services/report-an-infringement**
