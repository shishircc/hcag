# Recreation centres for migrant workers

Migrant workers may visit any of these recreation centres (RCs) to shop, play sports and attend events.

You may watch the video with [Chinese, Tamil, Bengali, Thai and Vietnamese subtitles](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLbLAc0yNACLMhpj23KqZyS8Ny1oylY_cJ).
