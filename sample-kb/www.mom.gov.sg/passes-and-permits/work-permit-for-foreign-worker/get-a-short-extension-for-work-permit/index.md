# Get a short Work Permit extension

You can request for a short Work Permit extension if you need more time before renewal or cancellation.

You can request for a short extension of your worker's Work Permit for up to 1 month if you:

- Need more time before you can renew the Work Permit, e.g. the worker is waiting for a new passport.
- Plan to cancel the Work Permit but need the worker to work for a short period beyond the expiry date.

Submit your request **within 2 weeks before the Work Permit expires**: 

1. Log in to [WP Online](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/wp-online-for-businesses-and-employment-agencies) .
2. Select **Special Pass/Short Work Permit Extension** on the left menu.

If you don't intend to keep employing your worker, please remember to [cancel the Work Permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/cancel-a-work-permit) **before the extended expiry date**. Otherwise, your security bond may be forfeited.
