# Calculate foreign employee quota

Use the quota calculator to plan how many Work Permit and S Pass holders your company can hire. For your company’s current workforce and quota, you should always check  [Work Permit Online](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/wp-online-for-businesses-and-employment-agencies).
