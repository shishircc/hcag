# Higher-skilled worker levy

Upgrading your worker to higher-skilled status will reduce your levy bill. The exact levy rate depends on your sector.

You pay a **lower levy** for workers with higher-skilled status.

## Criteria for higher-skilled worker levy by sector

The requirements your worker must meet to be on the higher-skilled levy rate are different for each sector:

## Apply for higher-skilled worker levy

### For construction sector

Your worker can get higher-skilled status by completing [one of the four upgrading pathways](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/higher-skilled-workers-for-construction-sector).

### For process sector

Your worker can get higher-skilled status through [one of the three upgrading pathways](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/higher-skilled-workers-for-process-sector).

### For manufacturing, marine shipyard or services sector

You need to [apply to convert your worker to higher-skilled levy](https://go.gov.sg/convert-skilled-levy), and upload a copy of the worker’s educational or trade test certificates.
