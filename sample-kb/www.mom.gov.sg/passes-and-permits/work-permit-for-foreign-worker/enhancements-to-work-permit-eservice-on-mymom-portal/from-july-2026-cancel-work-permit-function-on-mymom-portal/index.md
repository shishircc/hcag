## Cancel Work Permit

## Request to cancel Work Permit

- 
  - 
  ### Before 20 July 2026
    - Log in to Work Permit (WP) Online to cancel your worker’s Work Permit.
    - If required, submit supporting documents using a separate online form.
 ### From 20 July 2026
    - Log in to [*myMOM* Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-workers-employed-by-businesses) to cancel your worker’s Work Permit.
    - If required, upload supporting documents within the same transaction.

## View cancellation outcome

- 
  - 
  ### Before 20 July 2026
    - Requests that **require** supporting documents may take up to 3 days to approve. Log in to WP Online to view the outcome.
    - For requests that **do not require** supporting documents, you can view the outcome using WP Online after 30 minutes.
 ### From 20 July 2026
    - Outcome of requests to cancel Work Permit is immediately available after completing the cancellation process on [*myMOM* Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-workers-employed-by-businesses) .
    - You will also receive an acknowledgement letter to your email address that you provided.

## Request for Special Pass or Special Pass extension

If more time is needed to get Work Permit issued or send worker home:

### Before 20 July 2026

- Use WP Online to request for a Special Pass or Special Pass extension
- If further extension is required, submit request with supporting documents (where applicable) via a separate online form

### From 20 July 2026

- Use [*myMOM* Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-workers-employed-by-businesses) to request for a Special Pass or Special Pass extension
- If further extension is required, use *myMOM* Portal to submit request with supporting documents (where applicable)

## Give consent for transfer of worker

### Before 20 July 2026

- Receive email or SMS notification to give consent for transfer of worker to another employer.
- Log in to WP Online to indicate if you are giving consent for the transfer.

### After 20 July 2026

- Log in to access the new function on [*myMOM* Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-workers-employed-by-businesses) to indicate if you are giving consent for the transfer.
