# Enhancements roadmap: Work Permit eService on myMOM Portal

*myMOM*Portal in 2026 and 2027, and how this will make your transactions simpler and more seamless.



### Since 2025: Apply & Appeal functions

You can submit Work Permit applications and appeals using *myMOM* Portal’s streamlined, user-friendly interface that guides you through every step.

[Apply for a Work Permit (for businesses) / Appeal against a rejected application](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-workers-employed-by-businesses)



### From May 2026: Issue function

You **no longer need to**:

- Separately update your migrant worker's address and mobile number via OFWAS before getting the Work Permit issued.
- Upload hard copy medical reports or chest x-rays. Your medical service provider will submit the medical results directly to MOM, saving you time and effort.

**For Medical Service Providers:** From end April 2026, you can directly submit workers’ medical examination (ME) results electronically through our [ME Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/medical-examination-portal), and no longer need to arrange for employers to collect the hard copy ME reports from you.

[Access support resources to ensure a smooth transition during the launch](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/enhancements-to-work-permit-eservice-on-mymom-portal/from-may-2026-issue-work-permit-function-on-mymom-portal)



### From second half of 2026: Cancel function

You will be able to experience simplified Work Permit cancellation processes.



### From first half of 2027: Renew function

Medical results would be submitted by your medical service provider, just like the issue function, meaning less task for you



### By end of 2027: Enhancements completed

You will be able to perform all Work Permit transactions in one place within *myMOM* Portal.

### Help shape the future of our Work Permit eServices

These improvements are guided by feedback from users like you. [Join our Work Permit user panel](https://go.gov.sg/user-panel-website-roadmap) to influence future enhancements and stay informed on upcoming features.
