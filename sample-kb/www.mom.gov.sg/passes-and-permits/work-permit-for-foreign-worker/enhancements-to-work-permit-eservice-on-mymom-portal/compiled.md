# Enhancements to Work Permit eService on myMOM Portal

- 
## From July 2026: Cancel Work Permit function on myMOM PortalResources for employers and employment agencies to cancel Work Permits, request for Special Passes, and for current employers to provide consent for migrant workers to transfer to a new employer.
- 
## From May 2026: Issue Work PermitResources for employers, employment agencies and medical service providers on the process to issue Work Permits for migrant workers.
- 
## Enhancements roadmap: Work Permit eService on myMOM PortalOverview of enhancements coming your way in 2026 and 2027.
- 
## Join our Work Permit user panelHelp shape the future of Work Permit digital services.

# Enhancements to Work Permit eService on myMOM Portal

    Building on the introduction of Apply and Appeal functions in 2025, Work Permit transactions for migrant workers will continue moving from Work Permit Online to 

*myMOM*Portal through 2027, allowing you to perform transactions on one platform more seamlessly.
