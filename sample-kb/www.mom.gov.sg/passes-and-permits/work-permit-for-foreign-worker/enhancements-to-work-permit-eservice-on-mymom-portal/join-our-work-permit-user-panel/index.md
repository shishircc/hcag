# Join our Work Permit user panel

We have exciting enhancements coming to our digital services to make your Work Permit transactions simpler and more seamless. Learn more about the [roadmap of enhancements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/enhancements-to-work-permit-eservice-on-mymom-portal/enhancements-roadmap) to our eService for Work Permit transactions. 

To ensure these improvements are designed with you in mind, we are committed to ongoing efforts to understand your feedback and experiences.

## How to join our Work Permit user panel

[Register your interest](https://go.gov.sg/user-panel-website) to be part of an exclusive group of users who would: 

- Directly influence the design of our Work Permit digital services by participating in upcoming research activities.
- Test new features before they launch.
- Be informed of briefings and updates on upcoming enhancements.

We will match you to relevant research activities based on your preferences and profile when available.
