# From May 2026: Issue Work Permit function on myMOM Portal

### Before 4 May 2026



#### Step 1

1. Collect completed hard copy ME report from medical service provider (MSP).
2. Update worker’s address and mobile number on Online Foreign Worker Address Service (OFWAS).



#### Step 2

Wait for at least an hour before logging in to Work Permit Online (WP Online).



#### Step 3

Upload worker's ME report and get the Work Permit issued on WP Online.

### From 4 May 2026, after the new Issue Work Permit function has been rolled out

You no longer need to:

- Separately update your worker's address and mobile number in OFWAS before getting the Work Permit issued.
- Upload hard copy MEs or chest x-rays. Your MSP will submit the medical results directly to us, saving you time and effort.



#### Step 1

**From 27 April 2026**: Receive email notification after MSP submits worker’s ME results to us. 



#### Step 2

**From 4 May 2026:**  Log in to access [new issue function](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-workers-employed-by-businesses) to:

- View worker’s ME results.
- Update migrant worker’s address and mobile number.
- Issue the Work Permit.

If you need more details on any abnormalities, arrange with MSP to receive full ME report.

### Before ME Portal enhancement



#### Step 1

Medical service provider (MSP) completes worker’s hard copy ME report.



#### Step 2

MSP arranges for employer to collect worker’s hard copy ME report.



#### Step 3

Employer uploads ME report to get Work Permit issued on Work Permit Online.

### From 27 April 2026, after ME Portal enhancement

Instead, submit workers’ ME results electronically through the ME Portal. MOM will notify the employer when ME results are received.



#### Step 1

MSP completes worker’s hard copy ME report.



#### Step 2

MSP submits worker's ME results to MOM within 1 week of ME completion using the [ME Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/medical-examination-portal).

 

#### Step 3

Employer receives email notification after MSP submits ME results.

**From 4 May 2026:** Employer views worker's ME results and gets the Work Permit issued with the [new issue function](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-workers-employed-by-businesses).

If employers need more information on any abnormalities, they may request MSP to provide full ME report.
