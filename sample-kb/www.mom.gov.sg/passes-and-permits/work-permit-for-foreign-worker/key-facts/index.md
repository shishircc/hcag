# Key facts on Work Permit for migrant worker

Get an overview and key facts about the Work Permit for migrant workers.

## Pass facts

| Who it is for | Workers from approved source countries/regions working in the construction, manufacturing, marine shipyard, process or services sector. Find out the [eligibility criteria](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules) . | 
|---|---|
| Who can apply | An employer or appointed employment agent needs to [apply for the permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/apply-for-work-permit) . | 
| Minimum/maximum age | At least 18 years old, and below 62 years old when applying for the Work Permit. | 
| Qualifying salary | No minimum required. | 
| What is the duration | Up to 2 years, depending on the validity of the worker's passport, security bond and employment period. | 
| Is it renewable | Yes. Find out [how to renew](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/renew-a-work-permit) . | 
| Passes for family | Not available. | 
| Quota and levy | Yes. Employers are limited by a quota for their industry and have to pay a monthly levy for each worker. Find out the [quota and levy requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/foreign-worker-levy) . | 
| Medical insurance | Employers must provide [medical insurance](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/medical-insurance) | 
| Primary Care Plan (PCP) | Employers must complete **all** of the following: | 
| Relevant legislation | [Conditions of Work Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/services-forms/passes/wpspassconditions.pdf)[Employment of Foreign Manpower Act](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/EFMA1990) | 
| Related eServices and forms | Access [online services and forms](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/eservices-and-forms) for Work Permit. | 

## Pass map

Get an idea of what you need to do before, during and after you apply for a Work Permit.

## Before applying

- 
  - 
 Understand the [general requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/work-permit-conditions) and[sector-specific rules](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules) to apply.
  - 
 Understand the foreign worker [quota and levy requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/foreign-worker-levy) .
  - 
  If you have never applied for a Work Permit, you need to [declare your business activity](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/declare-your-business-activity) .*For first-time employers*
- 

## Before worker's arrival

- 
### Apply for Work Permit
  - 
  [Apply for the Work Permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/apply-for-work-permit) . Once approved, you will get an in-principle approval (IPA) to bring the worker into Singapore.
- 
- 
### Prepare for worker's arrival
  - 
 Ensure that the worker complies with the latest [travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore) .
  - 
  Book a slot at the [Onboard centre](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/onboard-centre) before securing your worker's flight.*For all non-Malaysian male CMP Work Permit holders on IPA*
  - 
  Buy a [security bond](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/security-bond) .*For non-Malaysian Work Permit holders*
  - 
 Buy [medical insurance](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/medical-insurance) for each Work Permit holder and submit the insurance details online.
  - 
  Buy the [Primary Care Plan](https://www.mom.gov.sg/primary-care-plan) .*For workers who stay in dormitories or work in CMP sectors*
  - 
  Register the worker for the [Settling-in Programme (SIP)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/settling-in-programme#register-for-sip) once you know their arrival date.*For employers of all non-Malaysian Work Permit holders in the manufacturing sector and female CMP workers*
- 

## Upon worker's arrival

- 
### Before permit can be issued
  - 
  Send the worker to the [Onboard centre](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/onboard-centre) directly after they arrive in Singapore.*For employers of all non-Malaysian male CMP Work Permit holders on IPA*
  - 
  Send the worker to attend [SIP](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/settling-in-programme) within first 14 days of arrival in Singapore.*For employers of all non-Malaysian Work Permit holders in the manufacturing sector and female CMP workers*
  - 
 
  - 
 Send the worker for a [medical examination](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/medical-examination) by a Singapore-registered doctor**within 2 weeks** of arrival.
- 
- 
### Get the Work Permit issued
  - 
 When the worker arrives, you can [get the permit issued](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/apply-for-work-permit#get-the-permit-issued) and you will get a notification letter stating whether registration is required.
  - 
 If required, make an appointment for the worker to [register their fingerprints and photo](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/apply-for-work-permit#register-fingerprints-and-photo) .
  - 
  [Receive the Work Permit card](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/apply-for-work-permit#receive-the-card) at the address provided 4 working days after registration or document verification.
- 

## Monthly

- 
  - 
  [Pay the foreign worker levy](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/foreign-worker-levy/paying-the-levy) by GIRO.
- 

## As and when required

- 
  - 
  [Notify MOM](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/notify-mom-of-changes) if the company's or worker's information changes.
  - 
 You can [apply for levy waiver](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/foreign-worker-levy/apply-for-levy-waiver) in certain situations.
  - 
  [Renew the Work Permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/renew-a-work-permit) before it expires.
  - 
  [Apply for a replacement](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/replace-a-card) if the permit card is lost, stolen or damaged.
  - 
  [Cancel the permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/cancel-a-work-permit) if the worker no longer works for you.
  - 
 Cancel the worker’s PCP by contacting your [PCP Anchor Operator](https://www.mom.gov.sg/primary-care-plan/buying-a-pcp#find-your-workers-zone-and-anchor-operator) .
