# Cancel a Work Permit

You must cancel a Work Permit if your worker no longer works for you.

## At a glance

| When to cancel the Work Permit | Within 1 week after the [last day of notice](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/termination-of-employment/termination-with-notice#start-and-end-of-the-notice-period) . If your worker has left Singapore and will not return to work for you, you must cancel the Work Permit **within 1 week from the departure date** . You **do not** need to cancel the pass if:  **Note:** Your worker’s levy will be charged until 1 day before the pass cancellation. | 
|---|---|
| Who can cancel | Employer or employment agent | 
| How long it takes | Immediate | 
| When levy stops | 1 day before cancellation | 

**Note:** You will need to provide [additional information or documents](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/cancel-a-work-permit#required-information-or-documents) for your cancellation request if your worker has: 

- Gone missing.
- Has been arrested.
- Is helping the authorities in an investigation.
- Has passed away.

## Before cancelling

### Settle all outstanding employment matters

As an employer, you need to:

1. **Give your worker reasonable notice** on their upcoming repatriation.
2. **[Seek tax clearance](<https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/employers/tax-clearance-for-foreign-spr-employees-(ir21)/getting-tax-clearance-a-step-by-step-guide>)** from the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) at least 1 month before your worker’s last day of employment.
3. **Settle all outstanding employment issues** that your worker may raise, including salary payments.

### Arrange for repatriation

1. **Ensure your worker has a valid passport.**
2. **D****iscuss and agree in writing** with your worker on the following before buying an air ticket:
  - Transit arrangements, such as connecting flights and layover hours
  - The allowance needed to sustain your worker during the transit
3. **Buy a one-way travel ticket** with a departure date within 14 days from the Work Permit cancellation.

**Note:**

For air travel, provide baggage allowance and cover all connecting transport costs to the international port of entry in your worker’s home country or the region nearest to their hometown.

## Required information or documents

You will need to provide information or documents for the following scenarios:

| If the worker | You will need to provide | 
|---|---|
| Is leaving Singapore | A scheduled departure date within 14 days of the Work Permit cancellation. | 
| Has gone missing | Missing person police report (required only for non-Malaysian workers). If the worker is not found within **1 month** from the date the Work Permit was cancelled, $2,500 (half of the $5,000 security bond) will be forfeited to cover the repatriation and other related costs. | 
| Has passed away |  | 
| Has been arrested or is helping authorities in an investigation | The investigating officer’s contact details. | 

## How to cancel

1. Log in to [*myMOM* Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-workers-employed-by-businesses) to cancel the Work Permit.
2. Print the Special Pass and give it to the worker. The stay duration is based on the scheduled departure date in *myMOM* Portal.
3. The worker needs to show the Special Pass and a valid passport to the immigration officer when leaving Singapore.

[request for a short extension](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/get-a-short-extension-for-work-permit)of the permit.

To avoid any [consequences of overstaying](#consequences-of-overstaying), you must ensure the worker leaves before the Special Pass expires. For non-Malaysians, the security bond will usually be discharged 1 week after the worker's departure, and if [all conditions](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/security-bond#when-will-the-bond-be-discharged) are met.

1. Log in to [*myMOM* Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-workers-employed-by-businesses) to cancel the Work Permit.
2. Select “Worker has already left Singapore” as the cancellation reason from the drop-down list.

You must cancel the Work Permit within 1 week of the departure date. For non-Malaysians, the security bond will be discharged within 2 weeks after we have verified that the worker has left Singapore, and if [all conditions](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/security-bond#when-will-the-bond-be-discharged) are met.

## After cancelling

If your worker is still in Singapore, your worker **cannot work**, even while waiting to leave Singapore. Your worker’s levy will be charged until 1 day before the cancellation.

Once the Work Permit is cancelled, please cut the card in half and discard it to prevent misuse.

If you have purchased a [Primary Care Plan](https://www.mom.gov.sg/primary-care-plan) (PCP) for your worker, you must terminate the PCP separately by contacting the [Anchor Operator](https://www.mom.gov.sg/primary-care-plan/buying-a-pcp#how-to-buy) directly. The PCP will not be automatically terminated when you cancel the Work Permit.

### Consequences of overstaying

Please ensure that your worker has valid legal stay throughout. Otherwise, an **overstaying fine** will be imposed. 

If the worker needs more time to leave Singapore, [request an extension of the Special Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/work-pass-general/how-can-i-extend-my-pass-holders-stay-in-singapore) before it expires.

It is also a **criminal offence** for: 

- Foreigners to remain beyond the expiry date of a visa
- Companies to employ foreigners without a valid work pass
