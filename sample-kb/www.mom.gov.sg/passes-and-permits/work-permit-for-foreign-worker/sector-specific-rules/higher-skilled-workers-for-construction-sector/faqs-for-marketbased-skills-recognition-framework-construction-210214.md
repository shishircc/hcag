# **FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS FOR MARKET BASED SKILLS RECOGNITION FRAMEWORK (MBF) FOR THE CONSTRUCTION SECTOR** 

|**Q1**|**When will the MBF take effect?**|
|---|---|
|A1|The MBF will take effect from 1 August 2014, and will apply to all WPHs in the<br>Construction Sector who can meet the requirements.|
|**Q2**|**Why do we need to wait till August 2014 to enjoy the new upgrading**<br>**pathway?**<br>**Can it be implemented earlier?**|
|A2|MOM will need some time to make the system changes to implement the MBF.<br>We ask for your patience.|
|**Q3**|**How do I apply to upgrade my WPH if the worker has already met the**<br>**requirements?**<br>**Will the upgrading be done automatically?**|
|A3|The new requirements will be implemented from 1 August 2014, and will be<br>automatic for most WPHs if their employers regularly update their current<br>salaries.<br>i)**New Work Permit applications**<br>WPHs who met the requirements will be accorded ‘Higher Skilled’ (R1) status at<br>the point of work permit application.<br>ii)**Existing WPHs who meet the requirements**<br>They will be upgraded to ‘Higher Skilled’ (R1) status from 1 August 2014. This<br>is automatic based on the fixed monthly salary that the employer has provided to<br>MOM and no formal request is required from employers. This is possible only if<br>employers have updated the WPH’s monthly salary on the WPOL.<br>iii)**WPHs who met the salary criteria but do not have at least 6 years POE**<br>**in Singapore Construction industry**<br>The WPH will be upgraded to ‘Higher Skilled’ (R1) status when his/her period<br>of employment in the Singapore Construction industry reaches 6 years.<br>iv)**WPHs with 6 years POE in Singapore Construction industry and**<br>**employer wishes to increase fixed monthly salary to $1,600**|

|**Q4**|**If my WPH has already met the market-based criteria, will he be upgraded**<br>**to ‘Higher Skilled’ (R1) immediately?**<br>**When will I get to enjoy lower levy?**|
|A4|The WPH will be upgraded. If there is any change in levy because of the ‘Higher<br>Skilled’ (R1) status, it will commence on the 1<sup>st</sup>day of the following month from<br>his / her ‘Higher Skilled’ (R1) status assigned date.|
|**Q5**|**How do I check if my existing WPH meets the 6 years Construction**<br>**experience requirement?**|
|A5|You can find these details in your Work Permit Online (WPOL) account. We<br>strongly encourage you to do so before submitting the WP applications.|
|**Q6**|**If my WPH attains ‘Higher Skilled’ (R1) in this month, when can I start to**<br>**enjoy the lower levy rate?**|
|A6|You will enjoy the lower levy rate in the same month that your WPH attained the<br>‘Higher Skilled’ (R1) status.<br>For example, if your WPH attains ‘Higher Skilled’ (R1) status on 1 August 2014,<br>you will be able to enjoy the lower levy rate from 1 August 2014 and view the<br>‘Higher Skilled’ (R1) status in the WPOL from 1 August 2014. However, as the<br>levy payable for the month is billed the following month, the August liability<br>showing the ‘Higher Skilled’ (R1) levy rate will only be reflected in the levy bill<br>that you will receive in September 2014.|
|**Q7**|**What proof do I have to show that my WPH is paid at least a fixed monthly**<br>**salary of $1,600?**|
|A7|You will need to declare the fixed monthly salary that your WPH is paid.<br>Making a false declaration is an offence and may subject you to penalties. We<br>will informyou if anyfurther verification documents are needed.|

|**Q8**|**If my WPH met the Market-Based Skills Recognition requirements and**<br>**obtained a ‘Higher Skilled’ (R1) status, but due to unforeseen**<br>**circumstances, his salary was reduced, will he still qualify for ‘Higher**<br>**Skilled’ (R1)? If not, will he be downgraded immediately?**|
|---|---|
|A8|Employers are required to declare to MOM if their employees’ salaries are<br>reduced. A failure to declare is an offence and may subject you to penalties. If<br>the WPH’s salary is reduced to less than $1,600 per month, he will no longer<br>qualify for ‘Higher Skilled’ (R1) under the MBF. The WPH will be considered<br>‘Higher Skilled’ if he meets the other skills upgrading pathways.|
|**Q9**|**Must the period of employment (POE) in Singapore Construction industry**<br>**be a continuous period? Or is it based on total number of years worked?**<br>**Are the POEs with previous companies and other sectors included in the**<br>**computation? If yes, is there a cap of the number of companies/sectors that**<br>**can be included?**|
|A9|The POE is computed by adding up the total number of years the WPH has<br>worked in Singapore,**as a Construction WPH**. There is**no restriction**on the<br>number of employers and**change of employments**in computing the POE.|
|**Q10**|**What is the definition for fixed monthly salary?**|
|A10|Fixed monthly salary is defined as the sum of basic monthly salary and fixed<br>monthly allowances.<br>Basic monthly salary means all remuneration payable monthly to a foreign<br>employee that does not vary from month to month on any basis, in respect of<br>work done under his contract of service.<br>Fixed monthly allowances means all allowances payable monthly to a foreign<br>employee that do not vary from month to month on any basis.<br>Please refer to the MOM Website for more details:<br>http://www.mom.gov.sg/foreign-manpower/passes-visas/employment-<br>pass/before-you-apply/Pages/fixed-monthly-salary.aspx|
