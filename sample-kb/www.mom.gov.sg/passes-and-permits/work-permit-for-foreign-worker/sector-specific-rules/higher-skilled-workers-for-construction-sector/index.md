# Higher-Skilled workers for construction sector

Higher-Skilled workers can enjoy lower levy rates.

## At a glance

| Criteria | CoreTrade | Multi-Skilling Scheme | Direct R1 Pathway | MBF | 
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Minimum years of experience** | 4 | 4-6 | No minimum | 6 | 
| **Skills or certification required** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | 
| **Minimum fixed monthly salary** | No minimum | No minimum | $1,600 | $1,600 | 

## 4 ways to upgrade to "Higher-Skilled"

You pay less levy for “Higher-Skilled” construction workers.

There are 4 ways to upgrade your construction workers from “Basic-Skilled” to “Higher-Skilled”:

- CoreTrade
- Multi-Skilling Scheme
- Direct R1 Pathway
- Market-Based Skills Recognition Framework (MBF)

## CoreTradeShow

### CoreTrade

| Years of experience in construction sector | Minimum 4 years | 
|---|---|
| Skills or certification required | CoreTrade skills assessment | 
| Minimum fixed monthly salary | No minimum | 
| More info | [BCA website](https://www1.bca.gov.sg/buildsg/manpower/construction-registration-of-tradesmen) | 

### How to upgrade your worker

1. [Register your worker with BCA](https://www.bca.gov.sg/cwrs/) for training and skills assessment.
2. If your worker passes the test, BCA will inform us and the new levy rate will take effect on the 1<sup>st</sup> of next month.
3. You will also see your worker’s new skill status and levy rate in [WP Online](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/wp-online-for-businesses-and-employment-agencies) .

2. [Register your worker with BCA](https://www.bca.gov.sg/cwrs/) as a**Multi-Skill worker** .
3. If the registration is successful, BCA will inform us and the new levy rate will take effect on the 1<sup>st</sup> of next month.
4. You will also see your worker’s new skill status and levy rate in [WP Online](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/wp-online-for-businesses-and-employment-agencies) .

## Direct R1 PathwayShow

### Direct R1 Pathway

| Years of experience in construction sector | No minimum | 
|---|---|
| Certification required | Depending on which country they are from, workers must have one of the following certificates: Malaysia  North Asian source (NAS)  Non-traditional source (NTS) and People’s Republic of China (PRC)  | 
| Minimum fixed monthly salary | $1,600 | 
| More info | [BCA website](https://www1.bca.gov.sg/buildsg/manpower/direct-r1-pathway) | 

- Malaysian and NAS construction Work Permit holders will qualify for the Direct R1 Pathway if they:
    
  - Earn a minimum fixed monthly salary of $1,600
  - Have one of the following:
        
    - SEC(K)
    - SPM (for Malaysians only)
    - End-of-course validation of the Accelerated Learning Programme (ALP)
  - Please refer to [BCA’s website](https://www1.bca.gov.sg/growth-and-transformation/manpower/built-environment-firms/workforce-development/upskilling-of-pmet-workforce/direct-r1-pathway/) for more information.

### How to upgrade your worker

1. Ensure your worker’s salary details in [WP Online](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/wp-online-for-businesses-and-employment-agencies) are updated.
2. [Register your worker with BCA](https://www1.bca.gov.sg/buildsg/manpower/direct-r1-pathway) Approved Training and Testing Centre or Overseas Testing Centre for the SEC(K) test at higher skill level.
3. If your worker passes the test, BCA will inform us, and the new levy rate will take effect on the 1<sup>st</sup> of next month.
4. You will also see your worker’s new skill status and levy rate in [WP Online](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/wp-online-for-businesses-and-employment-agencies) .

The R1 eligibility status of a worker is valid for 2 years. To renew the R1 eligibility or check the expiry date of their R1 eligibility, workers can log into the [BCA Construction Workforce Registration System (CWRS) – Direct R1 Renewal portal](https://eservices.bca.gov.sg/DirectR1/).
