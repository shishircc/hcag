# Work Permit: hiring an existing marine shipyard worker

You can hire existing workers, without them having to leave Singapore first. Find out the requirements and how to apply.

## At a glance

| Who are eligible | Existing marine shipyard workers from non-traditional source (NTS), North Asian sources or People’s Republic of China (PRC). **Note:** You can also find out about[hiring existing Malaysian workers](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/how-do-i-hire-an-existing-malaysian-work-permit-holder) . | 
|---|---|
| When you can start applying | **With the current employer's consent:** Anytime during the worker’s employment in Singapore. You can find out [more details](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/hiring-existing-worker-in-marine-shipyard-sector#apply-for-a-transfer-worker) . **Without the current employer's consent:** From **40 to 21 days before** their current Work Permit expires. | 
| How long it takes | [Within 1 week](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/work-pass-general/what-are-the-changes-in-the-processing-time-for-work-passes) . It will take longer if we need more information. | 

## Why hire an existing worker

You may consider hiring workers who are already working in Singapore to:

- Benefit from experienced workers and a faster deployment time.
- Save on the cost of bringing in new workers.

## Apply for a transfer worker

If you require prior approval (PA) for the Work Permit application, please choose PA type as “New” when you apply through [WP Online](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/wp-online-for-businesses-and-employment-agencies).

## With current employer's consent Show

**validity of 14 days or more**:

1. Submit your Work Permit application by following the general [Work Permit application process](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/apply-for-work-permit) . If the worker is eligible for transfer, we will send an SMS or email notification to the current employer.
 You should opt for email notifications during your application, so that you will receive:
  - A summary of your application.
  - Notifications to check your application status once there are updates.
2. Wait for the current employer to give consent. The current employer needs to log in to [*myMOM* Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/mymom-portal)**within 7 days** to approve or reject the transfer request. Otherwise, your Work Permit application will be rejected.
3. Check the application outcome by logging in to [*myMOM* Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/mymom-portal) > Work Passes > Quick menu > View details. You will also be notified by email if you had opted for it.

If you need an earlier transfer, have a discussion with the worker and current employer to come to an agreement on when to cancel the existing Work Permit

[apply for pre-approval](https://form.gov.sg/67fdce50ee9bdc752e718622)from MOM. You will need the current employer to sign the

[consent form](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/work-passes-and-permits/coe-consent-template.pdf).

## Without current employer's consentShow

To apply for a new Work Permit for the transfer:

1. [Apply for pre-approval](https://form.gov.sg/67fdd19e18c93d23a8acc82d)
2. Once pre-approval is obtained, you can [apply for a new Work Permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-workers-employed-by-businesses)
3. Upon approval, you need to get the Work Permit issued.

You can only request for the Work Permit to be issued when the **current permit has been cancelled or expires.**
