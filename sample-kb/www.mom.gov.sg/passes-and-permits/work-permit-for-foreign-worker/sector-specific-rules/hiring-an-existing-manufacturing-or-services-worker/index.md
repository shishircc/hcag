# 
        Hiring an existing manufacturing or services worker
    

You can hire existing workers, without them having to leave Singapore first. Find out the requirements and how to apply.

 
 
## At a glance

| Who are eligible | Existing manufacturing and services workers from North Asian sources, People's Republic of China (PRC) or Non-Traditional Sources (NTS). You can only hire from your own business sector. **Note:** You can also find out about[hiring existing Malaysian workers](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/how-do-i-hire-an-existing-malaysian-work-permit-holder) . | 
|---|---|
| When you can start applying | **With the current employer's consent:** Anytime during the worker’s employment in Singapore. You can find out [more details](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/hiring-an-existing-manufacturing-or-services-worker#apply-for-a-transfer-worker) . **Without the current employer's consent:** From **40 to 21 days before** their current Work Permit expires. You first need to[apply for pre-approval](https://form.gov.sg/67fdd19e18c93d23a8acc82d) from MOM. | 

## Why hire an existing worker

You may consider hiring workers who are already working in Singapore to:

- Benefit from experienced workers and a faster deployment time.
- Save on the cost of bringing in new workers. 

## Apply for a transfer worker

To apply for the transfer when the worker's Work Permit has a validity of 14 days or more:

1. Submit your Work Permit application by following the general [Work Permit application process](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/apply-for-work-permit) . If the worker is eligible for transfer, we will send an SMS or email notification to the current employer.You should opt for email notifications during your application, so that you will receive:   - A summary of your application. 
  - Notifications to check your application status once there are updates

 
Wait for the current employer to give consent. The current employer needs to log in to [*myMOM* Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/mymom-portal) **within 7 days** to approve or reject the transfer request. Otherwise, your Work Permit application will be rejected.
Check the application outcome by logging in to [*myMOM*](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/mymom-portal) [Portal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/mymom-portal) > Work Passes > Quick menu > View details. You will also be notified by email if you had opted for it.
If you need an earlier transfer, have a discussion with the worker and current employer to come to an agreement on when to cancel the existing Work Permit.

If the worker's Work Permit is expiring in less than 14 days, you first need to 

[apply for pre-approval](https://form.gov.sg/67fdce50ee9bdc752e718622)
 from MOM. You will need the current employer to sign the 

[consent form](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/work-passes-and-permits/coe-consent-template.pdf)
.

 When the workers’ Work Permits are within 40 to 21 days from expiry, you can apply for new Work Permits for them without their current employer’s consent.

To apply for a new Work Permit for the transfer:

1. [Apply for pre-approval](https://form.gov.sg/67fdd19e18c93d23a8acc82d)
2. Once pre-approval is obtained, you can [apply for a new Work Permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-workers-employed-by-businesses)
3. Upon approval, you need to get the Work Permit issued.

You can only request for the Work Permit to be issued when the 

**current permit has been cancelled or expires**
.

## As a current employer, what could I do to keep my workers

As a current employer, you are given priority to renew your workers’ Work Permits.

If you want to keep your workers, you should **renew their Work Permits early**, upon receiving the renewal notice. 

When your workers’ Work Permits are **within 40 to 21 days from expiry**, another employer can apply for new Work Permits for them without your consent.  

**If the new employer’s application is approved:**

- You can continue to employ the worker, as long as the existing Work Permit is valid.
- You are responsible for settling any outstanding salary while the worker was under your employment.
- You can refer to your[roles and responsibilities in the table above](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/hiring-an-existing-manufacturing-or-services-worker#roles-and-responsibilities) .
