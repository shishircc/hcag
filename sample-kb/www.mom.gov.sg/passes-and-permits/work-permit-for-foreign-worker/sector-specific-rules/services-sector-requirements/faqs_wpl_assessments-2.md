# **FAQS ON LITERACY ASSESSMENTS FOR WORK PERMIT HOLDERS IN THE SERVICES SECTOR** 

|**(A)**<br>**Q1**|**General FAQs**<br>**What tests are included in the Workplace Literacy (WPLN) assessment?**|
|---|---|
|A|The WPLN assessment comprises standalone tests for English literacy in<br>Reading, Listening, Speaking and Writing, as well as Numeracy. It will<br>produce separate results for each of these skills.<br>From 2 January 2020, the British Council will take over from the SkillsFuture<br>Singapore’s Centre of Employability Skills (CES) to conduct the WPLN<br>assessment. It will include an additional “Core Grammar and Vocabulary”<br>component.<br>Employers may visit British Council’s website<br>(https://www.britishcouncil.sg/exam/wpln)to find out more, or register for the<br>new WPLN assessment.|
|**Q2**|**Why should Work Permit holders take an English literacy assessment?**|
|A|While English literacy is not a mandatory requirement, the language is<br>commonly used in the Hotel, Retail and Food & Beverage (F&B) industries. It<br>is important that service staff can speak and understand simple English to<br>help them interact with customers.|
|**Q3**|**If Work Permit holders had taken the WPLN assessments earlier with**<br>**CES, are their results/WSQ SOAs still valid?**|
|A|The results of Work Permit holders who have taken the WPLN assessments<br>under the CES system (i.e. before 14 December 2019) will remain valid going<br>forward. There is no expiry to test-takers’ results or WSQ SOAs.<br>They can check their WPLN assessment results and certification using the<br>MySkillsFuture portal using the Work Permit holder’s FIN or other<br>identification.|
|**Q4**|**How can WPLN help Work Permit holders in the hotel, retail and F&B**<br>**industries qualify as higher-skilled workers?**|
|A|All Work Permit holders in the Services sector need to have relevant<br>academic or skill/trade qualifications to qualify for higher-skilled status.<br>Non-Malaysian Work Permit holders in the Hotel, Retail and F&B industries,<br>will also need to obtain level 4 of the WPLN listening and speaking<br>assessments, in addition to the those qualifications.|

## **Work Permits and who have met all other requirements for higherskilled status** 

Higher-skilled status will be automatically retained if the worker obtains level 4 of the WPLN assessments. Employers can register their workers who have received their Work Permit cards for the WPLN assessments. There is no need to inform MOM that the Work Permit holders have obtained level 4 of the WPLN assessments, as British Council will inform MOM of it within a month from taking the assessments. **Q9 Will there be any concession for new Work Permit holders that possess the relevant academic or skill/trade qualifications?** A New Work Permit holders that possess the relevant academic, skill or trade qualifications would be given a three-month provisional higher-skilled status. They must obtain level 4 of the WPLN assessments to retain the higherskilled status after the provisional higher-skilled status expires. **(B) English Language Courses to Prepare for  the WPLN Assessments Q1 Where can employers find information on training providers or programmes that they can send their workers to?** A Companies can send their Work Permit holders for English language courses or conduct their own training sessions should they wish to improve their workers' English language proficiencies. Employers can refer to British Council website at <u>https://www.britishcouncil.sg/exam/wpln</u> for details on the WPLN assessments. **Q2 Can employers pass the WPLN assessment costs on to their foreign workers?** A No. Under the Work Permit conditions, employers are prohibited from recovering employment-related costs from their workers. MOM will monitor and enforce against employers who attempt to recover employment-related costs from their foreign workers through salary deductions. Those who do so will be prosecuted and/or barred from hiring foreign workers. Any employer who contravenes the Work Permit conditions faces a fine of up to $5,000, and/or imprisonment of up to six months upon conviction.

|**(D)**|**Taking the WPLN Assessments**|
|---|---|
|**Q1**|**Will employers need to submit the results to MOM?**|
|A|No. British Council will send the results of the WPLN assessments to MOM.|
|**Q2**|**Do employers need to re-send a Work Permit holder to take the WPLN**<br>**assessments if the worker changes companies?**|
|A|No. The WPLN assessments results will continue to be valid even if the Work<br>Permit holder changes companies.|
|**Q3**|**Will the WPLN assessment results expire?**|
|A|No. There is no expiry date for the WPLN assessment results.|
|**Q4**|**What will happen to the Work Permit holders** **who fail the WPLN**<br>**assessments?**|
|A|Work Permit holders may attempt the WPLN assessments until they pass.<br>They are encouraged to attend English language training before their next<br>attempt at the WPLN assessments.<br>Their Work Permits will not be cancelled if they fail the WPLN assessments,<br>but they will not be given higher-skilled levy status after the three-month<br>provisional higher-skilled status.  Employers can still employ these Work<br>Permit holders, but they have to pay the higher basic-skilled levy.|
