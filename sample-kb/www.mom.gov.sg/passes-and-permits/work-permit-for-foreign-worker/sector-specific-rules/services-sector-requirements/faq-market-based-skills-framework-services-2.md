# **FAQS ON MARKET-BASED SKILLS FRAMEWORK FOR WORK PERMIT HOLDERS (WPH) IN THE SERVICES SECTOR** 

|**Q1**|**How do I apply to upgrade my WPH if I know he has already met the**<br>**requirements? Will the upgrading be done automatically?**|
|---|---|
|A1|The new requirements will be implemented on 1 Jul 2013, and will be automatic<br>for most WPHs if their employers regularly update their current salaries. To help<br>employers prepare for the implementation, you may wish to take note of the<br>following:<br>**i) New**WP applications<br>WPH who met the requirements will be accorded R1 status at the point of Work<br>Permit application depending on the tier the WPH is in.  If the WPH is assigned<br>in the tier where there is skilled levy differentiation, he/she will be assigned the<br>lower levy rate. Employers are strongly advised to check/verify the WPH’s POE<br>via the Work Permit Online (WPOL) before submitting the WP applications.<br>**ii) Existing**WPH who meet the requirements<br>They will be upgraded to R1 (skilled) worker status on 1 Jul 2013. This is<br>automatic and no formal request is required from employers. This is possible<br>only if employers constantly update MOM on the WPH’s monthly salary.<br>iii) WPH who met the salary criteria but do not have at least 4 years POE<br>WPH will be upgraded to R1 status when his/ her period of employment reaches<br>4 years.<br>iv) WPH with 4 years POE and employer wishes to increase salary to $1,600<br>WPH will be upgraded to R1 on the 1<sup>st</sup>of the following month that employer<br>update MOM the WPH’s salary. Employers will need to update the monthly<br>salary information via the “Change Worker’s Salary” module in the Work Permit<br>Online. In order for timely assignment of WPH skilled worker status, employers<br>are advised to update any revision of monthly salary promptly. As any upgrade<br>is done once a month on the 1<sup>st</sup>of each month, any delay in revising the<br>monthly salary information will delay the assignment to skilled worker status.|
|**Q2**|**If my WPH has already met the market-based criteria, will he be upgraded**<br>**to R1 immediately? When will I get to enjoy lower levy?**|
|A2|WPH will be upgraded. If there is any change in levy because of the skilled<br>worker status, it will commence 1<sup>st</sup>of the following month from his/her R1<br>(skilled) status assigned date.|
|**Q3**|**My current WPH is a R1 because he has the relevant academic**<br>**qualification (or passed the trade test/WSQ Composite Assessment). If**<br>**now he does not meet the market-based requirements, will he be**<br>**downgraded?**|
|A3|No, because the market-based skills framework supplements the current skills<br>framework. The WPH will still continue to be assigned the skilled worker status|

|**Q4**|**How do I check if my existing WPH meets the 4 years working experience**<br>**requirement?**|
|A4|You can find these details in your Work Permit Online (WPOL) account. We<br>strongly encourage you to do so before submitting the WP applications.|
|**Q5**|**I am planning to hire a WPH under R1. Is there a way that I can verify if he**<br>**has worked in Singapore as a WPH for at least 4 years?**|
|A5|Refer to A4.|
|**Q6**|**If my WPH attains R1 in this month, when can I start to enjoy the lower**<br>**levy rate?**|
|A6|If your WPH attains R1 on 1 July 2013, you will be able to enjoy the lower levy<br>rate from 1 July 2013 and view the R1 status in WPOL from 1 Jul 2013.<br>However, as levy payable for the month is billed the following month, the July<br>liability showing the R1 levy rate will only be reflected in the levy bill that you will<br>receive in August.|
|**Q7**|**Will I enjoy the lower levy for my WPHs in Tier 3 if they meet the new**<br>**criteria for R1?**|
|A7|No. Currently, there is no skilled levy rate for WPHs in Tier 3 (a flat rate applies<br>for Tier 3). A skilled levy rate will be introduced for Tier 3 in Jul 2014. Your<br>WPHs in Tier 3 will enjoy the skilled rate from Jul 2014 if they meet the R1<br>criteria.|
|**Q8**|**What proof do I have to show that my WPH is paid at least fixed monthly**<br>**salary of $1,600?**|
|A8|You will need to declare that your WPH is paid that amount of salary. There is a<br>penalty for false declaration. We will inform you if any further verification<br>documents are needed and will release any details at a later date.|
|**Q9**|**If my WPH meet the market-based requirements and obtain a R1, but due**<br>**to unforeseen circumstances, his salary has to be reduced. Will he still**<br>**qualifies for R1? If not, will he be downgraded immediately?**|
|A9|Employers need to declare to us if their employees’ salaries are reduced. If the<br>WPH’s salary is reduced to less than $1,600 per month, he will no longer qualify<br>for R1.|

|A10|The POE is computed by adding up the total number of years WPH has worked<br>in Singapore with valid work permits from the first employment. It is company or|
