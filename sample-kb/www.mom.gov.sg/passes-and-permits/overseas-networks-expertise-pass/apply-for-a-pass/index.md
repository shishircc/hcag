## Submit an applicationShow

*Processing time: 4 weeks for most cases*

### How to apply

1. Prepare the [documents required](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/documents-required) .
2. Complete the [online application form](https://service2.mom.gov.sg/workpass/op/landing) .
3. Pay the $105 application fee via Visa or Mastercard.

[required documents](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/documents-required)are properly completed before submission.

### How to check your application status

You may [check your application status online](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/check-work-pass-and-application-status) after 4 weeks.

If additional information or documents are required:

- Your processing time may take longer than 4 weeks
- We will email you (please reply directly to our email)

### Receiving your application results

We will send your results to the **email** indicated in your application form.

If your pass is approved, you should also receive an IPA letter. Your IPA letter:

- **Is a pre-approved single-entry visa for you to enter Singapore.** It gives you 6 months to enter Singapore and get your pass issued.
- **States whether you need to go for a medical examination** after your arrival in Singapore.

 **During application**, you should: 

1. Inform the candidate of the:
    
  - Contents of the application.
  - Documents submitted as part of the application.
  - Declaration made as part of the application.
2. Send a copy of the submitted application form to the candidate for their reference.

 **After the application has been approved**, the pass holder will need to:

1. Sign the relevant declaration form.
2. Submit it during his or her pass issuance appointment at the Employment Pass Services Centre.

## Prepare for arrival in SingaporeShow

### Before departing

You should:

- Print your IPA letter.
- Ensure that you comply with the [latest travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore) .

### After arriving

You should:

- Show your IPA letter to the immigration officer.
- Complete any required medical examinations.

## Get the pass issuedShow

*When: Within 6 months of IPA*

*Processing time: Immediate*

You must be in Singapore to get your pass issued.

| If you | Please do so | 
|---|---|
| Have a Singpass account | In [EP eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) | 
| **Do not** have a Singpass account | At Employment Pass Services Centre (EPSC) | 

### EP eService

For candidates with Singpass accounts to get the pass issued:

1. [Log in to our eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) and provide the[required information and documents](#what-you-need) .
2. Pay the fees using GIRO, Visa, Mastercard or Amex: 
    
  - $225 for each pass
  - (If applicable) $30 for each [Multiple Journey Visa](https://www.ica.gov.sg/#/visitor/visitor_entryvisa)
3. Once the pass is issued, you will receive a **notification letter** by email.

### EPSC

For candidates without Singpass accounts, you can get your pass issued over the counter at our EPSC. Please refer to the following steps:

1. [Make an appointment](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/make-an-appointment) to have your pass issued at our[Employment Pass Services Centre](https://www.mom.gov.sg/contact-us/locations#Tab-EP) .
2. Bring the [supporting documents](#what-you-need) for your appointment.
3. Pay the fees using VISA, MasterCard, AMEX, NETS or CashCard (if your credit or debit card was not issued in Singapore, please ensure it has been activated for overseas use):
    
  - $225 for each pass
  - (If applicable) $30 for each [Multiple Journey Visa](https://www.ica.gov.sg/#/visitor/visitor_entryvisa)
4. You will be issued a **notification letter** .
