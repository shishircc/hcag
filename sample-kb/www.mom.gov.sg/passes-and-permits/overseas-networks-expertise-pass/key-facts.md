## Features of the pass

The key features of the pass include the following:

- Exemption from Complementarity Assessment Framework (COMPASS) and Fair Consideration Framework job advertising requirements
- Flexibility to concurrently start, operate and work for multiple companies at any one time (subject to your employment contract terms, where relevant)
- No need to reapply for a pass if you change jobs
- Availability of [passes for eligible family members](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families)
- Your spouse will be able to apply to work on a [Letter of Consent](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families/dependants-pass-for-overseas-networks-expertise-pass/working-in-singapore) .

You may be required to pay [income tax](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/tax-residency-and-tax-rates/working-out-my-tax-residency).

## Pass map

Overview of what you need to do before, during and after you apply for an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass.

## Before applying

- 
  - 
 Know the [eligibility and requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/eligibility) to apply.
  - 
 Find out the [documents required](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/documents-required) for applying.
- 

## Before arrival

- 
  - 
  [Apply for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass) and wait for the outcome.
  - 
  (If your pass is approved) Get an in-principle approval letter.
  - 
 Ensure that you comply with the latest [travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore) .
- 

## Upon arrival

- 
  - 
  [Get your pass issued](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass#get-the-pass-issued) and receive a notification letter stating whether registration is required.
  - 
 (If required) Make an appointment for the candidate to [register fingerprints and photo](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass#register-fingerprints-and-photo) .
  - 
  [Receive your pass card](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass#receive-the-card) at the address provided 5 working days after registration or document verification.
- 

## As and when required

- 
  - 
  [Notify MOM](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/notify-mom) about your professional activities and annual income by 31 January of the next year.*You should also notify MOM about any changes to your residential address or personal information.*
  - 
  [Apply for a replacement](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/replace-a-pass-card) if your pass card is lost, stolen or damaged.
  - 
  [Cancel your pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/cancel-a-pass) if you no longer require it.
