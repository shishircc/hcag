# Are there any additional conditions that I need to meet for certain occupations?

In addition to obtaining an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass, you will require professional registration from the respective professional bodies.

Examples of such professions include healthcare professionals, lawyers, football players or coaches.

If your employer is a food establishment, you may also require licensing, such as a valid license issued by the Singapore Food Agency (SFA).

<!-- source: can-a-non-publicly-listed-company-be-considered-established-based-on-company-valuation.md -->
# Can a non-publicly listed company be considered established company based on company valuation instead of market capitalisation?

No, we do not consider company valuation as an alternative to market capitalisation.

Non-publicly listed companies must meet the annual revenue criterion of at least US$200 million to be considered established companies as they do not have market capitalisation.

<!-- source: can-a-spouse-of-an-onepass-holder-apply-for-multiple-loc.md -->
# Can a spouse of an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass holder apply for multiple Letters of Consent (LOC)?

    No. A spouse can only hold one LOC at a time, as they are only allowed to work for one employer. If the spouse owns a business, they are considered employed by their own company and cannot apply for an additional LOC to work for another employer.

<!-- source: can-i-apply-for-a-one-pass-if-i-hold-another-work-pass-or-if-i-am-overseas.md -->
# Can I apply for an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass if I hold another work pass or if I am overseas?

Yes, candidates who are existing work pass holders or who are currently overseas are able to apply.

You should only have your work pass cancelled after your [Overseas Networks and Expertise Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass) application is approved and when you are ready to get the pass issued. Do note that once your work pass is cancelled, you are not permitted to work until you get your Overseas Networks and Expertise Pass issued

<!-- source: can-i-apply-for-the-one-pass-from-outside-singapore.md -->
# Can I apply for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass from outside Singapore?

Yes, you can [apply for the pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass) while you are outside Singapore. If you are successful in your application, you will have to make an appointment to get the pass issued at the [Employment Pass Services Centre](https://www.mom.gov.sg/contact-us/locations#Tab-EP) in Singapore.

You can refer to the in-principle approval (IPA) Letter for more information.

# Can I authorise a third-party or employment agency to submit the annual notification for Overseas Networks and Expertise Pass on my behalf?

The annual notification can be submitted by:

- The Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass holder
- An employment agency appointed by the pass holder
- The pass holder’s employer

Only one submission is required for each pass holder. If we receive more than one submission from or on behalf of the pass holder, the latest submission will replace the previous submission.

<!-- source: can-i-keep-my-one-pass-if-i-am-leaving-singapore-for-good-but-my-pass-has-not-expired.md -->
# Can I keep my Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass if I am leaving Singapore permanently but my pass has not expired?

No. If you are no longer working in Singapore and you are leaving Singapore permanently, you will have to [cancel the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/cancel-a-pass).

<!-- source: can-i-stay-in-singapore-while-unemployed-if-i-hold-a-one-pass.md -->
# Can I stay in Singapore while unemployed if I hold an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass?

Overseas Networks and Expertise Pass holders are given the flexibility to explore new opportunities. We understand that this could take time. Therefore, you do not need to immediately leave Singapore if you become unemployed.

However, we will [request annual information](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/notify-mom#annual-notification) on your professional activities and income over the past year. This is to ensure that you are contributing meaningfully and to assess your [pass renewal](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/eligibility) after 5 years.

Your pass status may be affected if you have long periods of unemployment without a valid reason.

<!-- source: can-i-submit-applications-for-my-familys-passes-together-with-my-application-for-the-onepass.md -->
# Can I submit application(s) for my family's passes together with my application for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass?

Yes, you can. However, we recommend applying for your family’s passes once your pass gets approved, as Dependant’s Pass and Long-Term Visit Pass applications take 4 weeks to process.

If your application as the main pass holder is rejected, your family’s pass application(s) will also be rejected. Fees paid for the application(s) are non-refundable.

# Can spouses of Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass holders on Letter of Consent be both business owners and employees?

No. A spouse can only hold one LOC at a time, as they are only allowed to work for one employer. If the spouse owns a business, they are considered employed by their own company and cannot apply for an additional LOC to work for another employer.

<!-- source: does-my-prospective-employer-need-to-meet-the-criteria-for-an-established-company.md -->
# Does my prospective employer need to meet the criteria for an established company?

Your prospective employer needs to be an [established company](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/eligibility#definition-of-an-established-company).

If not, you may [re-apply for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass) after you have earned a [fixed monthly salary](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/employment-pass/what-is-a-fixed-monthly-salary) of at least S$30,000 for the12 consecutive months leading up to the date of application. 

Alternatively, you may apply for another work pass, such as the [Employment Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass), [Personalised Employment Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass), [EntrePass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/entrepass) or [Tech.Pass](https://www.edb.gov.sg/en/how-we-help/incentives-and-schemes/tech-pass.html).

# I am an existing Employment Pass holder converting to the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass. Do I need to re-apply for my family's passes?

Yes, you will need to submit new applications.

We recommend applying for your [family’s passes](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families) once your pass gets approved. Dependant’s Pass (DP) and Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) applications take 4 weeks to process.

If your application as the main pass holder is rejected, your family’s pass application(s) will also be rejected. Fees paid for the application(s) are non-refundable.

# I am an existing pass holder, and I am converting to the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass, do I need to re-apply for passes for my dependants?

Yes, you will need to submit new applications.

We recommend applying for your family’s passes once your pass gets approved, as Dependant’s Pass (DP) and Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) applications take 4 weeks to process.

Although you can apply for your family’s passes at the same time as the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass, should your application as the main pass holder be rejected, your family’s pass application(s) will also be rejected. Fees paid for the application(s) are non-refundable.

# If I have worked in Singapore for less than 12 months, can I apply for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass as an existing work pass holder?

If you have worked in Singapore for less than 12 months, you may apply under the overseas applicant route and submit information on your overseas employment before coming to Singapore. We will consider your employment records over the 12 consecutive months leading up to the date of application when assessing your [eligibility for an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/eligibility).

Otherwise, you may apply for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass after you have worked in Singapore and earned a fixed monthly salary of at least S$30,000 for the 12 consecutive months leading up to the date of application.

# If my Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass application is unsuccessful, will I be automatically assessed for an Employment Pass?

No, you will not be automatically assessed for an Employment Pass if your Overseas Networks and Expertise Pass application is unsuccessful. Your employer should [submit a separate application for the Employment Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass) if you meet its [eligibility requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility).

# I will be starting my employment in a few weeks’/months’ time. When can I apply for an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass?

You may [submit your application](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass) for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass no earlier than 6 months before the employment starts. The processing time for an application is 4 weeks for most cases. It may take longer if additional information is required.

# I am an existing Employment Pass (EP) holder. Should I cancel my EP if I want to apply for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass?

No, you do not need to cancel your existing pass to apply for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass. You should ask your current employer to [cancel your EP](https://safe.menlosecurity.com/https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/cancel-a-pass) only **after** your Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass application is approved and when you are ready to get the pass issued.

Do note that once your EP is cancelled, you are not permitted to work until you get your Overseas Networks and Expertise Pass issued.

# Will my spouse lose their existing Work Permit if I change from an Employment Pass to an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass?

Yes. Your spouse's existing Work Permit will no longer be valid as it is [tied to their Dependant’s Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/working-in-singapore) (DP). Once your [Employment Pass (EP) is cancelled](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/cancel-a-pass), the associated family passes will also be cancelled. 

To avoid this, you should only cancel your EP **after** your [application for an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass) is successful.

You will need to reapply for a [DP](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass) for your spouse once your Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass is approved as it takes 4 weeks to process. Your spouse should make the necessary arrangements for his/her work for this transition.
