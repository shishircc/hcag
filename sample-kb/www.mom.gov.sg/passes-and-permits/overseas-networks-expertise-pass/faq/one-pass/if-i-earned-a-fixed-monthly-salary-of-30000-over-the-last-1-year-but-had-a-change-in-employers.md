If I earned a fixed monthly salary of S$30,000 over the last 1 year but had a change in employers, am I eligible for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass?

Yes, you are still eligible if you change employers. You will also need to earn a fixed monthly salary of S$30,000, from any one employer, for the last 12 consecutive months leading up to the date of application.
