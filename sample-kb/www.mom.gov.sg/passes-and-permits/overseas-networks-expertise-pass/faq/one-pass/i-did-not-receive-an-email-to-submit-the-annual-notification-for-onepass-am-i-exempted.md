# I did not receive an email to submit the annual notification for Overseas Networks and Expertise Pass. Am I exempted?

You only need to submit your annual notification if you receive an email from MOM. MOM will inform Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass holders who are required to submit their annual notifications via the email contact details provided during the pass application.

To ensure that we can reach you in the future, please log in to [Employment Pass eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) with your Singpass to update your contact details.
