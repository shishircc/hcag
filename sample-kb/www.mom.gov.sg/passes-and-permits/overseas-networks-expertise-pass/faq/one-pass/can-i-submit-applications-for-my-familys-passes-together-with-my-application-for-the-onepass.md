# Can I submit application(s) for my family's passes together with my application for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass?

Yes, you can. However, we recommend applying for your family’s passes once your pass gets approved, as Dependant’s Pass and Long-Term Visit Pass applications take 4 weeks to process.

If your application as the main pass holder is rejected, your family’s pass application(s) will also be rejected. Fees paid for the application(s) are non-refundable.
