# Does my prospective employer need to meet the criteria for an established company?

Your prospective employer needs to be an [established company](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/eligibility#definition-of-an-established-company).

If not, you may [re-apply for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass) after you have earned a [fixed monthly salary](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/employment-pass/what-is-a-fixed-monthly-salary) of at least S$30,000 for the12 consecutive months leading up to the date of application. 

Alternatively, you may apply for another work pass, such as the [Employment Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass), [Personalised Employment Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass), [EntrePass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/entrepass) or [Tech.Pass](https://www.edb.gov.sg/en/how-we-help/incentives-and-schemes/tech-pass.html).
