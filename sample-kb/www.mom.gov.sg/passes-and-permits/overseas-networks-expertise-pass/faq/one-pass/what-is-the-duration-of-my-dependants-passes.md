# I am an Overseas Networks & Expertise pass holder. What is the duration of my dependants' passes?

The duration is tied to the validity of the main pass holder’s pass which is valid up to 5 years. 

If you successfully renew your Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass, your family's passes can also be renewed if they meet the [criteria](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families).
