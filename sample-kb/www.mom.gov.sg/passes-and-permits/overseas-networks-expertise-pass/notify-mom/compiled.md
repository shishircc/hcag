# Notify MOM of updates: Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass

If you hold an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass, find out about the annual notification and how to notify us of changes to your details.

## Annual notification

The annual notification exercise allows us to understand the contributions of Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass holders in Singapore. As a pass holder, you may be required by MOM to participate in an annual notification exercise where you will need to provide the following information:

- Employment details, such as your employer(s) and occupation(s)
- Total annual income for the past calendar year
- Other professional activities, if applicable

Pass holders who need to submit the annual notification will receive an email with further instructions.

## Residential address or mobile numberShow

You need to update us **within 2 weeks** of changing your residential address or mobile number. Otherwise, you may be penalised.

**How to update:**

1. (For address updates) Ensure that your new address meets the relevant [housing requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/housing/various-types-of-housing) .
2. Log in to [EP eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) to update your address or mobile number. The update is immediate.
3. Print the acknowledgement page and keep it with your pass card.

## Personal informationShow

### Changes to passport number and expiry date

You can update your passport number and expiry date instantly using [EP eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice). Written requests to update these two details will no longer be accepted.

If you wish to clear immigration using the automated gates at local checkpoints, you can do so

**4 working days**after making the update.

## Restricted occupations for the Overseas Networks and Expertise Pass

If you are a Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass holder, you cannot work in certain restricted occupations, such as media or religion related occupations, unless you have obtained approval from MOM.

To work in a restricted occupation, you are required to submit a request to MOM using our [online form](https://go.gov.sg/request-restricted-occupation). You can only start work in a restricted occupation after your request has been approved. You will receive a response from MOM within 3 weeks in most cases.

Please refer to the [full list of restricted occupations](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/work-passes-and-permits/ove/restricted-occupations-for-ove.pdf).
