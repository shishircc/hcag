## Features of the pass

The key features of the pass include the following:

- Exemption from Complementarity Assessment Framework (COMPASS) and Fair Consideration Framework job advertising requirements
- Flexibility to concurrently start, operate and work for multiple companies at any one time (subject to your employment contract terms, where relevant)
- No need to reapply for a pass if you change jobs
- Availability of [passes for eligible family members](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families)
- Your spouse will be able to apply to work on a [Letter of Consent](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families/dependants-pass-for-overseas-networks-expertise-pass/working-in-singapore) .

You may be required to pay [income tax](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/tax-residency-and-tax-rates/working-out-my-tax-residency).

# 
        Eligibility for Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass
    

    Requirements include a fixed monthly salary of S$30,000 under an employer for the 12 consecutive months leading up to the date of application or prospective employer based in Singapore, or outstanding achievements in certain sectors.
    

 
## Who is eligible

We assess Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass candidates on a case-by-case basis. Choose the category that best fits you to learn about the eligibility requirements. You must meet all the criteria within your chosen category to be eligible.

You will be applying based on:

- Earned a [fixed monthly salary](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/employment-pass/what-is-a-fixed-monthly-salary) of at least S$30,000, or its equivalent in foreign currency, for the 12 consecutive months leading up to the date of application.
- Worked for an established company overseas (if applicable) for 12 consecutive months leading up to the date of application.
    

For a company to be considered established, it must have at least one of the following: 

- Market capitalisation of at least US$500 million 
- Annual revenue of at least US$200 million 

Combined amounts from the entire global office can also be considered, and will be assessed on a case-by-case basis.

 
 
Will earn a [fixed monthly salary](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/employment-pass/what-is-a-fixed-monthly-salary) of at least S$30,000 under an established company that is based in Singapore.

For a company to be considered established, it must have at least one of the following: 

- Market capitalisation of at least US$500 million
- Annual revenue of at least US$200 million

Combined amounts from the entire global office can also be considered, and will be assessed on a case-by-case basis.

 
When applying based on prospective employment in Singapore, submit your application **only** when your employment will start within 6 months.

 
You may apply without meeting the salary criteria if you have outstanding achievements in at least one of the areas below:

- Sports
- Arts and culture
- Academia and research

MOM and other agencies such as MCCY, MOE, NRF and A*STAR will holistically review applications based on the work and key achievements accomplished by each applicant.

Learn more about the [documents required for outstanding achievements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/documents-required).

 
If you drew salaries from more than one employer in the 12 consecutive months leading up to the date of application, your qualifying [fixed monthly salary](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/employment-pass/what-is-a-fixed-monthly-salary) of at least S$30,000 should come from one employer only. 

Other sources of income may be considered on a case-by-case basis.

Existing pass holders need to have worked in Singapore for at least 1 year.

# Documents required for Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass

## Required documents

All Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass applications need to include a **passport page**, including any amendments.

If the candidate's name in their passport is different from their name in other documents, you also need to upload:

- An explanation letter
- Supporting documents, such as a deed poll

## Additional documents by category

Choose the category which best describes the candidate to find out the documents required.

## Current or last held employment in Singapore Show

You will need to submit the following documents

- **Monthly payslips** for the 12 consecutive months leading up to the date of application

- **Most recent Notice of Assessment**  from the[Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/receive-tax-bill-pay-tax-check-refunds/getting-my-tax-assessment)  - If you have not received your Notice of Assessment (NOA) from the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS), you may provide the latest Income Tax Return filed with IRAS instead, i.e. Form B1.
- **Documents supporting other income sources** other than fixed monthly salary (if applicable)

## Current or last held employment overseas Show

You will need to submit the following documents:

- **Monthly payslips** for the 12 consecutive months leading up to the date of application

- **[Employment verification proof](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/work-pass-general/what-are-the-requirements-for-a-verification-proof#background-screening-companies)** from a background screening company
- **Documents supporting other income sources** other than fixed monthly salary (if applicable)
- **Latest financial information** of the candidate’s employer obtained within the last 3 months. This includes documents indicating the latest market capitalisation or annual company revenue. You may get this information from any of the following sources:

If this information is not available, you may provide the company’s latest audited Profit & Loss statements instead. MOM will only consider documents from third party auditors. Documents signed by the company’s management staff will not be accepted.

## Prospective employment in SingaporeShow

You will need to submit the following documents:

- **Signed employment contract with a Singapore organisation** or a letter of offer from a prospective employer in Singapore.
- **Latest financial information** of the candidate’s prospective employer obtained within the last 3 months. This includes documents indicating the latest market capitalisation or annual company revenue. You may get this information from any of the following sources:

If this information is not available, you may provide the company’s latest audited Profit & Loss statements instead. MOM will only consider documents from third party auditors. Documents signed by the company’s management staff will not be accepted.

## Outstanding AchievementsShow

You will need to submit the following documents.

- **Payslips** from the latest month
- **Documents supporting other income sources** other than fixed monthly salary (if applicable)
- **Curriculum Vitae (CV)** highlighting key achievements

Candidates applying under the academia and research track should also include:

- **A CV, which lists significant publications** , patents and technology disclosures achieved in the past 5 years.
- **A recommendation letter or endorsement letter** , or a document for a potential position by a local host institution. Examples of local host institutions include[Autonomous Universities](https://www.moe.gov.sg/post-secondary/overview/autonomous-universities) or[A*STAR](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/) .

Find out how the [fixed monthly salary](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/employment-pass/what-is-a-fixed-monthly-salary) is defined.

# Appeal against a rejected Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass application

## When to appeal

You should only submit an appeal for an unsuccessful Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass application if you can **address the issues in your rejection advisory**. There will be no change in outcome if you submit the same information in the appeal.

You need to submit your appeal **within 3 months** of the rejection. Otherwise, you will have to submit a new application. 

Alternatively, you may wish to apply for a different work pass to work in Singapore, such as: 

## Who can submit the appeal

The following parties can submit an appeal:

- The candidate
- Employment agent acting on behalf of the candidate
- Employer acting on behalf of the candidate 
 

## Submit an appeal

You can [submit your appeal online](https://form.gov.sg/60bf57b5f9b60d0011ab6c5d).  

Authorised employment agents must attach a signed authorisation letter stating that the candidate has authorised you to submit an appeal on their behalf. 

## How long it takes

Most appeals will be processed **within 4 weeks**. We will send the outcome to the email address provided in the original application form.

Once the pass is cancelled:

- You will not be able to reinstate it.
- All related [passes issued to family members](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families) will also be cancelled. You will not be able to reinstate them.

## Before cancelling

Your employer must seek [tax clearance](<https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/employers/tax-clearance-for-foreign-spr-employees-(ir21)/getting-tax-clearance-a-step-by-step-guide>) from the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) at least 1 month before you leave the job.

If you are self-employed, you must [fulfil your tax obligations](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/self-employed-and-partnerships/tax-obligations-of-foreigners-ceasing-business-or-leaving-singapore-permanently) at least 1 month before your expected date of departure.

## How to cancel

Log in to [EP eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) on *myMOM* Portal to cancel the pass. 

For pass holders who are still in Singapore, you can **request for an STVP** in the eService to grant yourself a valid stay for up to 90 days from the date of application.

You will receive the STVP by email. You need to show the STVP and a valid passport to the immigration officer when leaving Singapore.

Once the pass is cancelled, please cut the pass card in half and discard it to prevent misuse.

You can collect your card at the EPSC 4 working days after your replacement card request is approved. You **do not** need to make an appointment.
