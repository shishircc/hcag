# Passes for families

## Who is eligible

Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass holders may apply for passes to allow the following family members to join them in Singapore.

### Spouse

| Family member | Pass type | 
|---|---|
| **Spouse** (legally married) | [Dependant’s Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families/dependants-pass-for-overseas-networks-expertise-pass) Spouses with Dependant's Passes can apply for a Letter of Consent (LOC) to              [work in Singapore](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families/dependants-pass-for-overseas-networks-expertise-pass/working-in-singapore) . | 
| **Common-law spouse**  (A common-law relationship between 2 people that is recognised under the laws of their country or region) | [Long-Term Visit Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families/long-term-visit-pass-for-overseas-networks-expertise-pass) Common-law spouses on LTVP can apply for a Letter of Consent (LOC) to [work in Singapore](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families/long-term-visit-pass-for-overseas-networks-expertise-pass/working-in-singapore) . | 

### Children and parents

| Family member | Pass type | 
|---|---|
| **Children** - unmarried and under 21 years old, including those legally adopted | [Dependant’s Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families/dependants-pass-for-overseas-networks-expertise-pass) | 
| **Handicapped children** - unmarried and aged 21 and above **Stepchildren** - unmarried and under 21 years old **Parents** of the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass holder | [Long-Term Visit Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families/long-term-visit-pass-for-overseas-networks-expertise-pass) | 

Passes for family are not available for children who are married or over the age or 21. They may choose to obtain their own [work pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits) if they wish to work in Singapore.

## When to apply

We recommend applying for your family’s passes once your pass gets approved, as Dependant's Pass and Long-Term Visit Pass applications take 4 weeks to process.

Although you can apply for your family’s passes at the same time as the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass, you may lose the application fees for each family pass if your Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass application gets rejected.
