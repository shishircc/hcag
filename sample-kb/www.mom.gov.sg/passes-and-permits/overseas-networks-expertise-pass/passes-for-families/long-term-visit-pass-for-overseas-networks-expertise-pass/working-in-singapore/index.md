# Working in Singapore for Long-Term Visit Pass holders of Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass holders

To apply for a Letter of Consent, the prospective employer is required to complete the following steps on behalf of the common-law spouse:

1. Download and fill in the [Letter of Consent (Common-Law Spouse) application form](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/services-forms/passes/loc_cls_application_form.pdf) .
2. [Submit](https://go.gov.sg/mom-submit-loc-cls) the application form with the relevant supporting documents online.

Other Long-Term Visit Pass holders **who are not common-law spouses** will need to obtain a work pass to work in Singapore.  

You first need to get a job offer in Singapore, then get your prospective employer to apply for an [Employment Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass), [S Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass) or [Work Permit](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker) for you. You will have to meet all the criteria for the pass that your employer is applying for.
