- **long**: This folder serves as the top-level reference for Singapore's Employment Pass (EP), which allows foreign professionals, managers, executives and technicians to work in Singapore with a minimum salary of $5,600/month. Details the application steps (download form, submit online), the full LOC application form fields (employer details, applicant personal/employment info, salary, qualifications, vetting agencies, declarations), required supporting documents, and the ~8-week processing time. Non-common-law-spouse LTVP holders must instead obtain a standard work pass (EP, S Pass, or Work Permit).
