# How do I cancel an Employment Pass (Sponsorship)?

If the pass holder is in Singapore, you can [make an appointment](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/make-an-appointment) with our Employment Pass Services Centre (EPSC) to cancel the Employment Pass (EP).

If the pass holder had left Singapore, you need to [request to cancel the EP](https://www.mom.gov.sg/cancel-sponsor-ep) .
