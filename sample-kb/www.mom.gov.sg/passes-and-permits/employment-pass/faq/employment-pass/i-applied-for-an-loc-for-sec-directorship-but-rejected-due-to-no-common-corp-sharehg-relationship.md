# I applied for a Letter of Consent (LOC) for secondary directorship. Why was I rejected due to there being no common corporate shareholding relationship?

You are only eligible for secondary directorship if both the primary and secondary companies have a common company shareholder. Do note that **this common shareholder must be a company**.

If both the primary and secondary companies are only linked by you as an **individual shareholder**, you will not be eligible for the LOC.

If there are any changes to the common corporate shareholding relationship, you may [submit another request](https://go.gov.sg/apply-secondary-directorship) for our assessment.
