# 
        COMPASS C5. Skills bonus - Shortage Occupation List (SOL)
    

    Employment Pass (EP) candidates filling roles on the Shortage Occupation List (SOL) can qualify for points under the 

[C5. Skills bonus](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility#c5-skills-bonus-shortage-occupation-list)
.
    

 
## What is the SOL 

The SOL lists all jobs eligible for the [COMPASS C5. Skills bonus](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility#c5-skills-bonus-shortage-occupation-list).

The jobs under the [infocomm technology sector](#tab-4) also qualify for the [5-year duration EP](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/experienced-tech-professionals-with-skills-in-shortage).

To obtain the C5. Skills bonus or a 5-year duration EP:

- Your candidate needs to perform the job duties listed for the specific shortage occupation.
- Your candidate needs to meet the additional requirements as stated in the [SOL Employer Guide](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/work-passes-and-permits/compass/sol-guidebook.pdf) .
- You need to select the shortage occupation in your EP application.

Learn more about: 

The SOL is created by MOM and the Ministry of Trade and Industry (MTI) in consultation with sector agencies and tripartite partners.

Occupations are identified for the SOL based on:

- Their strategic importance to Singapore’s economic priorities
- The degree and nature of labour shortage
- The sector’s commitment to developing the local pipeline to address these shortages in the medium term

 
To obtain C5. Skills Bonus points or qualify for a 5-year duration EP:

- Candidates should select one of the eligible job titles in the EP application form. This must be the same as the occupation for the job advertisement in [MyCareersFuture](https://www.mycareersfuture.gov.sg/) , unless you are[exempted from the FCF job advertising requirement](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/consider-all-candidates-fairly#exemption-from-advertising-requirement) .
- Your application needs to meet the additional requirements specific to the shortage occupation. This can include:
      - Relevant qualifications, to be uploaded under the qualifications section of the EP application form
  - Work experience in relevant job titles, to be selected under the work experience section in the EP application form
  - Registration proof, to be uploaded in the agency support question in the EP application form

 ## Updates to the SOL

Occupations may be added or removed from the SOL **once a year**, to remain responsive to market conditions. 

 The SOL will be comprehensively reviewed every 3 years.
