# Notify MOM of updates: Employment Pass

If you’re an EP holder or employer, find out when and how to notify MOM of changes, including updates to company name and address, occupation, salary and residential address.

You will need to notify us in these situations:

## Change in pass holder's salaryShow

*Who does this: Employer*

As an employer, you must notify us if you lower or raise a pass holder's salary.

### If you lower the salary

You can [submit a request](https://go.gov.sg/mom-update-salary) **at least 1 month** before the date you intend to lower the salary.

The pass holder must continue to qualify for the work pass that he is holding with the revised salary. You may use the [Self-Assessment Tool (SAT)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-s-pass-self-assessment-tool) to find out if they are likely to meet the qualifying salary.Otherwise, you should cancel the work pass and apply for a new work pass based on his eligibility.

### If you raise the salary

You don't need to inform us immediately unless you are applying for dependant privileges.

Instead, you can indicate the change in salary when you [renew the EP](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/renew-a-pass).

#### Applying for dependant privileges

Pass holders who have an increase in salary must [notify MOM](https://go.gov.sg/mom-update-salary) before applying for dependant privileges.

## Change in company's name or financial informationShow

*Who does this: Employer*

### Update company's name or paid-up capital

[Unique Entity Number (UEN) issuance agency](https://www.uen.gov.sg/ueninternet/faces/pages/admin/aboutUEN.jspx)), your records will be automatically updated in EP eService within 2 weeks. You don’t need to update MOM separately.

However, if you need to perform transactions with us urgently, you can update your company’s name or paid-up capital using our [online form](https://go.gov.sg/update-company-name-puc).

If your company has a CPF account, you should also [inform the Central Provident Fund (CPF) Board](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/employer/making-cpf-contributions/updating-employer-s-particulars) on the name change.

### Update company turnover

You must provide your company’s turnover information for the past 3 years before you can apply for or renew a pass in [EP eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice).

| Employment agent helping a client who does not have an EP eService account to provide the information | Submit a [request](https://go.gov.sg/update-turnover) . |

## Change in company addressShow

*Who does this: Employer*

You can update your company address using [EP eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice). This will ensure that you continue to receive important messages and correspondences from us.

[update the Central Provident Fund (CPF) Board](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/employer/making-cpf-contributions/updating-employer-s-particulars)about the change.

## Change in business entityShow

*Who does this: Employer*

You need to inform us if your company is undergoing the following types of changes that will result in the change in the CPF submission numbers:

- Changing to another type of business entity (e.g. from a sole proprietorship to a private limited company), where at least one of the directors, partners or sole proprietor will move to the new entity.
- Undergoing full merger, acquisition or amalgamation of entities.
- Renewing a business registration with a different [ACRA](http://www.acra.gov.sg) number while keeping the company name

For these above scenarios, find out [how to transfer your workers to the new company](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/work-pass-general/how-do-i-transfer-my-companys-work-pass-holders-if-it-is-undergoing-business-restructuring). 

## Transfer of pass holder to a related companyShow

*Who does this: Current employer*

An Employment Pass holder can be [transferred to a related company in Singapore (i.e. branch, subsidiary, or affiliated company)](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/work-pass-general/can-my-company-be-exempted-from-fcf-advertising-requirements-is-candidate-is-a-local-transferee). The current employer will need to follow these steps:

1. Get the related company to [apply for a new Employment Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass) for the pass holder. The related company doesn't need to advertise the position on MyCareersFuture.
2. [Cancel the existing Employment Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/cancel-a-pass) when the employment ends. Cancellation must be done before the related company can get the new Employment Pass issued.

[changing your business entity](#change-in-business-entity).

## Change in pass holder's occupationShow

*Who does this: Employer*

To update a pass holder's occupation:

1. [Submit a request](https://form.gov.sg/5d8b04900c67f000120d2a1f) .
2. Choose the closest match to the new occupation, from the A-Z list of standard occupations: [A-I](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/services-forms/passes/list_of_standard_occupation_a-i.pdf) ,[J-R](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/services-forms/passes/list_of_standard_occupation_j-r.pdf) ,[S-Z](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/services-forms/passes/list_of_standard_occupation_s-z.pdf) .
3. (For [some occupations](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/documents-required#for-healthcare-professionals-lawyers-football-players-or-coaches) ) You need to upload support letter from the respective professional bodies.

We will email you the outcome within 3 weeks.

## If a pass holder goes missingShow

*Who does this: Employer*

You must make a missing person police report and [cancel the Employment Pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/cancel-a-pass) within 1 week of knowing that the pass holder is missing.

## Change in pass holder's residential address or mobile numberShow

*Who does this: Pass holder or employer*

Either party needs to update us **within 5 days of changes** to the residential address or mobile number. Otherwise, the EP holder may be penalised. 

**How to update:**

1. (For address updates) Ensure that the new address meets the relevant 
    [housing requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/housing/various-types-of-housing) .
2. Log in to [EP eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) to update the address or mobile number. The update is immediate.
3. Print the acknowledgement page and keep it with the pass card.

## Change in pass holder's personal informationShow

*Who does this: Pass holder or employer*

### Changes to passport number and expiry date

You can update your passport number and expiry date instantly using [EP eService](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice). Written requests to update these two details will no longer be accepted.

**4 working days**after making the update.

### Changes to other particulars

[Submit your request online](https://go.gov.sg/update-passholder-particulars), together with the required supporting documents.

## Change in application details after pass is approvedShow

[online form](https://form.gov.sg/#!/617687029982c20014043c87)to inform MOM if there is a change in the pass holder’s following information (after the work pass has been approved):

- Educational qualification
- Work location

## Provide a reference for your pass holderShow

You can [submit a request](https://go.gov.sg/mom-referee-request) to be a referee for your ex-work pass holder after you have cancelled the work pass.

Upon receiving your request, we will:

- Inform the prospective employer or employment agent who is applying for a work pass for the worker that you have offered to provide a personal reference check.
- Release your contact details so that they can contact you to find out more about the worker’s conduct, character, or work performance, if they wish to do so.

The prospective employer or employment agent may also speak to the foreigner directly before deciding if they want to proceed to apply for the work pass.

This request will be valid for 2 years, but you can submit a request to withdraw at any time.

We are also unable to act based on non-employment related feedback from landlords, housing agents etc. They can choose to either seek legal advice, file a police report, or settle the matter directly with the foreigner.
