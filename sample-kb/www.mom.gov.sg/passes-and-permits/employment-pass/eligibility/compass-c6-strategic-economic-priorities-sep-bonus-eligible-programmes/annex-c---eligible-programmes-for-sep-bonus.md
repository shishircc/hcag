# **Annex C – Eligible programmes for Strategic Economic Priorities bonus** 

To be eligible for the Strategic Economic Priorities (SEP) bonus, firms must be supported by economic agencies or NTUC. To be considered, firms must participate/meet the criteria for at least one of the following programmes/categories below. More details are on MOM’s website – SEP bonus.
