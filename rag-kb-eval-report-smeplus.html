## COMPASS Overview

**COMPASS** (Complementarity Assessment Framework) is Singapore's points-based assessment system for Employment Pass (EP) applications. It was introduced on **1 September 2023** for new applications and applies to renewals for passes expiring from **1 September 2024** onwards.

### Purpose

COMPASS is a transparent system designed to:
- Give businesses greater clarity and certainty for manpower planning
- Enable employers to select high-quality foreign professionals
- Improve workforce diversity
- Build a strong local core workforce

### Two-Stage Eligibility Framework

To qualify for an EP, candidates must pass:

1. **Stage 1**: Meet the EP qualifying salary (minimum $5,600–$10,700+ depending on age and sector)
2. **Stage 2**: Pass COMPASS (unless exempted) by earning at least **40 points**

### Six COMPASS Criteria

COMPASS evaluates candidates across six criteria, divided into **individual attributes** and **firm-related attributes**:

#### **Individual Attributes** (Candidate-focused)
- **C1. Salary** (0–20 points): Candidate's salary benchmarked against local PMET salaries in the sector
- **C2. Qualifications** (0–20 points): Based on degree-equivalent qualifications from top-tier or other recognized institutions

#### **Firm-Related Attributes** (Organization-focused)
- **C3. Diversity** (0–20 points): Whether the candidate's nationality improves the firm's nationality diversity among PMET employees
- **C4. Support for Local Employment** (0–20 points): Based on the firm's local PMET share relative to its sector

#### **Bonus Criteria**
- **C5. Skills Bonus** (0–20 points): For candidates in shortage occupations on the Shortage Occupation List (SOL)
- **C6. Strategic Economic Priorities Bonus** (0–10 points): For organizations participating in eligible government programmes

### Exemptions from COMPASS

Candidates are exempted if they meet **any** of these conditions:
- Fixed monthly salary of at least **$22,500**
- Applying as an overseas intra-corporate transferee
- Filling the role for 1 month or less

### Tools Available

- **Workforce Insights Tool (WFI)**: Check your organization's scores on firm-related attributes (C3 and C4)
- **Self-Assessment Tool (SAT)**: Predict whether an application will meet COMPASS requirements before submission
- **COMPASS Guidebook**: Detailed guidance on navigating the framework
