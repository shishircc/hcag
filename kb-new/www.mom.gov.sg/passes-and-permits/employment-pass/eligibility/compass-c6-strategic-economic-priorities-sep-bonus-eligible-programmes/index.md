# COMPASS C6. Strategic Economic Priorities (SEP) bonus - eligible programmes

[C6. SEP bonus](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility#c6-strategic-economic-priorities-bonus), your organisation needs to participate in at least one of the eligible programmes.

## Eligible SEP programmes

Your organisation needs to participate in at least **one of the following** programmes to be considered for the [C6. SEP bonus](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility#c6-strategic-economic-priorities-bonus).

- Download the [eligible SEP programmes](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/press-releases/2023/annex-c---eligible-programmes-for-sep-bonus.pdf) .
- Contact the supporting agencies directly if you have any queries.
